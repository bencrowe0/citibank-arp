Thursday, April 16, 2026 at 8:15 a.m. ET

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • Chairman and Chief Executive Officer — Ramon Laguarta
  • Chief Financial Officer — Stephen Schmitt

TAKEAWAYS

  • Core Operating Margin -- Increased approximately 10 basis points, with underlying expansion masked by a property sale gain effect in the Frito-Lay North America (PFNA) segment.
  • Organic Revenue -- Reported company-wide growth of 2.6%, demonstrating sequential improvement following prior quarters.
  • Core EPS -- Rose 9%, reflecting effective cost management and operational leverage.
  • PFNA Volume Growth -- Achieved 2% volume increase and 4% unit growth, with 300 million new consumption occasions year over year, which management attributed to innovation, brand restages, increased consumer value, and away-from-home acceleration.
  • North America Foods Cost -- Experienced a cost reduction, credited explicitly to productivity programs begun in early 2025.
  • Away-from-Home Foods Business -- Growing at three times the company average, supporting overall sector acceleration.
  • Permissible Foods Portfolio -- Select brands reported double-digit growth rates, indicating strong consumer uptake of "permissible and functional" offerings.
  • PFNA Innovation Penetration -- Management stated 40%-50% ACV currently, forecasting further acceleration for new products and planogram resets into summer.
  • North America Beverages (PBNA) Top Line -- Grew 9%, comprised of 2% organic revenue growth and an additional 7 percentage points from recent business integrations, including poppi and energy lines.
  • PBNA Volume Trend -- Segment posted nearly flat volume when excluding ongoing transition of case pack water to a third party, with volume expected to turn positive after full transition lapses next quarter.
  • Market Share (PFNA) -- Management indicated recent gains in both volume and value share, using IRI data as reference.
  • International Segment -- No observable demand impact attributable to the Iran conflict; acceleration continues, bolstered by competitive supply chain positioning.
  • Guidance -- Remains 2%-4% organic revenue growth for the year, with the potential for upper-range performance in the second half under current assumptions.
  • Productivity Initiatives -- Ongoing cost savings efforts include automation, AI integration, SKU reduction, plant closures, digitization, supply chain optimization, and targeted advertising efficiency, with plans for further expansion in future quarters.
  • Innovation Contribution (PFNA) -- Significant portion of new growth attributed to recent launches including protein and fiber product lines, with growing retail space gains expected through the summer.

RISKS

  • Chief Financial Officer Stephen Schmitt cited inflation as a material uncertainty, stating, "our assumption is that inflation will come. The order of magnitude, we're still working through, and I think a lot of that is still to be determined."

SUMMARY

The call revealed that PepsiCo (PEP +0.45%) drove sequential improvement in key metrics through productivity and innovation, while reaffirming organic revenue guidance and highlighting resilient international demand despite ongoing geopolitical uncertainty. Executives committed to margin flexibility across business units to sustain investment and emphasized the continued effectiveness of hedging programs in mitigating near-term cost volatility. New distribution gains, category expansion, and the ongoing World Cup activation were cited as incremental drivers for the balance of the year, particularly in international and emerging markets.

  • Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Ramon Laguarta described a multiyear journey to deepen supply chain digitization, AI-driven efficiency, and integrated global innovation execution.
  • Management's scenario planning for 2027 earnings is underway but no new detail was disclosed or incorporated in present-year guidance.
  • PFNA's holistic business transformation included value-seeking consumers returning to core brands and lapsed households reentering the category due to targeted innovation and promotional execution.
  • Functional hydration and energy segments, notably CELSIUS and expanded Gatorade offerings, were positioned as market share growth vectors within PBNA.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • ACV (All Commodity Volume): A measure of a product’s distribution, indicating the percentage of retail sales volume in stores where the product is available.
  • LRB (Liquid Refreshment Beverages): Industry term covering all ready-to-drink non-alcoholic beverages, including water, sports drinks, teas, and juices.
  • SKU (Stock Keeping Unit): A distinct item, such as a product or service, used for inventory tracking and sales analysis.
  • Planogram: A visual diagram or model that indicates product placement on retail shelves to maximize sales and display effectiveness.
