DATE

Wednesday, February 4, 2026 at 4:30 p.m. ET

CALL PARTICIPANTS

  • Chief Executive Officer — Sundar Pichai
  • President and Chief Investment Officer — Philip Schindler
  • Chief Financial Officer — Anat Ashkenazi

TAKEAWAYS

  • Consolidated Revenues -- $113.8 billion, representing 17% constant currency growth, driven by acceleration in Search and Cloud revenues.
  • Operating Income -- $35.9 billion, up 16%, with an operating margin of 31.6% reflecting a $2.1 billion stock-based compensation charge linked to Waymo’s valuation increase.
  • Net Income -- $34.5 billion, up 30%, with earnings per share increasing 31% to $2.82.
  • Operating Cash Flow -- $52.4 billion in Q4, a company record, resulting in $24.6 billion of free cash flow for the quarter.
  • CapEx -- $27.9 billion in Q4 and $91.4 billion for the year, primarily allocated to technical infrastructure; 60% invested in servers, 40% in data centers and networking.
  • Google Services Revenue -- $95.9 billion, up 14%, with Search and Other Advertising revenues up 17% to $63.1 billion driven by broad vertical strength and especially retail.
  • YouTube Advertising Revenue -- $11.4 billion, up 9%, supported by direct response, while network advertising revenues declined 2% to $7.8 billion.
  • Subscriptions, Platforms, and Devices Revenue -- $13.6 billion, increasing 17% due to strong momentum in YouTube subscriptions and Google One, benefiting from AI plan demand.
  • Google Cloud Revenue -- $17.7 billion, accelerating 48%, with operating income of $5.3 billion and operating margin up from 17.5% to 30.1%.
  • Cloud Backlog -- $240 billion, up 55% sequentially and more than double year over year, driven by enterprise AI demand and multi-year customer commitments.
  • AI Adoption Metrics -- Gemini 3 Pro now processes three times as many daily tokens as its predecessor, with over 8 million paid Gemini Enterprise seats sold and the Gemini app surpassing 750 million monthly active users.
  • Efficiency Gains -- CEO Pichai said, "We were able to lower Gemini serving unit costs by 78% over 2025 through model optimizations, efficiency, and utilization improvements."
  • CapEx Guidance -- 2026 CapEx expected in the $175 billion to $185 billion range, primarily to support AI compute, technical infrastructure, and cloud growth.
  • Waymo -- Surpassed 20 million fully autonomous trips, now delivering more than 400,000 weekly rides, and recently launched service in its sixth market, Miami.
  • Shareholder Returns -- $5.5 billion in share repurchases and $2.5 billion in dividend payments were made during the quarter.

RISKS

  • Operating Expense Growth -- CFO Ashkenazi stated, "the significant increase in our investments in technical infrastructure will continue to put pressure on the P and L in the form of higher depreciation expense and related data centers operations costs such as energy."
  • CapEx and Supply Chain Uncertainty -- Ashkenazi explained, "the availability of supply, pricing of components, and timing of cash payments can cause some variability in the reported CapEx number."
  • AI Compute Capacity Constraints -- CEO Pichai said, "I do to go through the year in a supply constrained way," indicating persistent compute and infrastructure limitations may impact ability to fully capitalize on demand.

SUMMARY

Alphabet (GOOGL -1.29%) delivered record revenues, accelerated margin expansion in cloud, and materially increased operating and free cash flow. Management asserted AI-driven efficiency and adoption are driving unit economics improvement, with Gemini model usage and customer commitments reaching new highs. Newly announced Apple and Reliance Jio partnerships further extend Gemini’s technological and commercial reach, and CapEx guidance signals a continued scaling of technical infrastructure. Product launches, including Gemini 3 and Universal Commerce Protocol, are designed to monetize emerging behaviors in search and agentic commerce across global platforms.

  • Waymo’s scale reached over 20 million fully autonomous trips and more than 400,000 weekly rides, highlighting Other Bets’ operational growth.
  • Cloud backlog more than doubled year over year to $240 billion, suggesting sustained enterprise demand for AI-powered cloud solutions.
  • CFO Ashkenazi disclosed $2.1 billion in stock-based compensation tied to Waymo’s investment round, directly impacting quarterly results.
  • YouTube’s annual revenue surpassed $60 billion (ads plus subscription), and daily Shorts views exceeded 200 billion, representing increased user engagement and monetization opportunities.
  • Management confirmed no evidence of cannibalization between native Gemini app usage and core Google Search, instead describing “an expansionary moment.”
  • Gemini’s integration with external SaaS leaders was cited as enabling deep workflow enhancements and increased token usage, according to CEO Pichai.
  • Depreciation rose 38% to $21.1 billion from $15.3 billion due to elevated CapEx and is expected to accelerate further in 2026.
  • Capital returned to shareholders and significant cash balances reinforce flexibility to manage cyclical pressures and invest in growth initiatives.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • Gemini: Alphabet’s proprietary family of large multimodal AI models powering products across Search, Cloud, and consumer applications.
  • AI Mode: A conversational, AI-powered interface within Search and the Gemini app, offering expanded user interactions beyond traditional queries.
  • Universal Commerce Protocol: An open standard developed by Alphabet for real-time agentic commerce, enabling seamless transactions across partners and platforms.
  • AI Max: AI-driven ads and creative asset generation tool used by advertisers to improve campaign performance and reach new customers.
  • TPU: Tensor Processing Unit—custom AI accelerator hardware designed and manufactured by Alphabet to optimize large-scale AI training and inference.
  • Circle to Search: Google Search feature allowing users to search using gestures or images on Android devices without leaving an app.
  • Generative AI Models: Machine learning models capable of producing text, images, audio, or video, based on patterns learned from large datasets.
  • Shoppable Mastheads: Interactive ad format on YouTube enabling viewers to browse and purchase products directly from the video platform.
