Chief Executive Officer — Sundar Pichai

Risks

Chief Financial Officer Anat Ashkenazi stated, "year-over-year comparisons in advertising will be negatively impacted by the strong spend on U.S. Elections in 2024, particularly on YouTube."

Ashkenazi said, "we still expect to remain in a tight demand-supply environment in Q4 and 2026," referencing data center and server capacity deployments.

She added, "the significant increase in our investments in technical infrastructure will continue to put pressure on the P&L in the form of higher depreciation expenses and related data center operations costs such as energy."

Operating margin for Google Services declined year over year to 38.5%, as healthy revenue growth and continued expense efficiencies were offset by the impact of the EC fine.

Takeaways

Consolidated Revenue -- $102.3 billion, up 16% year over year or 15% in constant currency.

Net Income -- $35 billion, a 33% increase, with earnings per share up 35% to $2.87.

Operating Income -- $31.2 billion, up 9%; operating margin reached 30.5%, or 33.9% excluding the European Commission fine.

Free Cash Flow -- $24.5 billion free cash flow; $73.6 billion for the trailing twelve months, benefiting from recent tax changes on R&D expensing and increased operating cash flow.

Google Services Revenue -- $87.1 billion, increasing 14%, with Search and advertising revenues up 15% to $56.6 billion and YouTube advertising revenues up 15% to $10.3 billion.

Cloud Revenue -- $15.2 billion, growing 34%; operating income up 85% to $3.6 billion with a 23.7% segment margin, compared to 17.1% a year ago.

Cloud Backlog -- Increased sequentially by 46% to $155 billion, with a $49 billion rise quarter over quarter, driven primarily by enterprise AI demand.

AI Metric -- Gemini app surpassed 650 million monthly active users, with queries tripling from Q2 2025; Gemini models process 7 billion tokens per minute via API.

AI Mode in Search -- Globally rolled out across 40 languages, now serves over 75 million daily active users, with queries doubling over the quarter.

YouTube Live Event -- Achieved a new record with over 19 million concurrent viewers for a livestream, setting a platform benchmark during the exclusive NFL broadcast in the month preceding Q3 2025.

CapEx Guidance -- Increased 2025 CapEx forecast to $91 billion–$93 billion, up from previous $85 billion estimate, and signaled a "significant increase" in expected 2026 CapEx.

Legal Expense -- $3.5 billion charge related to a European Commission fine fully reflected in the Google Services segment.

Paid Clicks and CPCs -- Paid clicks rose 7% and cost-per-clicks grew 7% year over year.

AI Revenue Growth in Cloud -- Revenue from AI-based cloud products increased more than 200% year over year, with nearly 150 cloud customers each processing about 1 trillion tokens over the past twelve months.

Subscription Revenues -- Subscriptions, platforms, and devices grew 21% to $12.9 billion, led by YouTube and Google One subscriptions.

Summary

Alphabet (GOOG -0.97%) reported its first-ever $100 billion-plus quarter, highlighting double-digit growth across key business lines and significant momentum from generative AI adoption. Cloud performance drove a 34% revenue gain and an 85% boost in operating income as AI infrastructure and enterprise solutions accelerated customer wins and backlog expansion. The rapid scaling of Gemini, AI mode in Search, and AI-powered monetization products contributed to increased user engagement and improved monetization efficiency in both Search and YouTube. Management raised CapEx guidance for 2025 and projected further infrastructure investment in 2026, with tight supply-demand conditions expected to persist. The quarter reflects ongoing transformation from AI and cloud capabilities, but was offset in part by elevated legal and infrastructure-driven expenses, moderation in network advertising, and stated headwinds for future period advertising comparisons due to the U.S. Election cycle.

Sundar Pichai noted, "our first-party models like Gemini now process 7 billion tokens per minute via direct API use by our customers," emphasizing operational scale in AI.

Philipp Schindler said, "AI Max and Search is already used by hundreds of thousands of advertisers, currently making it the fastest-growing AI-powered search ads product."

Anat Ashkenazi stated, depreciation increased $1.6 billion year over year to $5.6 billion, reflecting a growth rate of 41%, underscoring the impact of capital investment on operating expenses.

YouTube generated an annual revenue run rate exceeding $1 billion globally for interactive direct response ads in the living room segment.

Philipp Schindler discussed that, "for AI overviews, even at our current baseline of ads, below and within the AI's response, overall, we see the monetization at approximately the same rate," suggesting sustained monetization efficiency from AI-driven search experiences.

Industry glossary

TPU (Tensor Processing Unit): A custom-developed ASIC by Google optimized for accelerating machine learning workloads, notably in AI model training and inference.

GCP (Google Cloud Platform): Alphabet's comprehensive suite of public cloud computing services.

AI Overview/AI Mode: Google Search features deploying generative AI to provide synthesized, multi-source answers and conversational capabilities for user queries.

AI Max: An AI-powered search ads product enabling advertisers to access and target expanded query surfaces using Google's advanced AI models.
