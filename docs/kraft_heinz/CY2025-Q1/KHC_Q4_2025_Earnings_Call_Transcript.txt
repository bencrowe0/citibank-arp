- Andre Maciel - Chief Financial Officer
- Anne-Marie Megela - Vice President, Investor Relations

KEY TAKEAWAYS:
- Capital Investment: Management committed an incremental $600 million to brand and commercial investment, with approximately half allocated to price, product, and packaging initiatives across the portfolio.
- Investment Ramp-up Timing: Steve Cahillane stated, "You're going to see the spend really start to ramp up in the second quarter," with meaningful results anticipated in the back half of the year.
- Separation Pause: Management announced a pause on the previously planned business separation, citing undisclosed opportunity to "return the company to organic growth" and preserve long-term portfolio optionality.
- North American Focus: The majority of new investment will target the U.S. grocery portfolio, while emerging markets and Canada are expected to continue delivering volume-led growth.
- Market Share Trends: Andre Maciel reported, "70% of the revenue in data elevation is now gaining market share," and U.S. market share has returned to levels from three years ago as of January reads.
- Opening Price Points: Approximately 40% of categories will have specific strategies for opening price points to address headwinds from reduced SNAP funding.
- SNAP Exposure: Andre Maciel disclosed, "about 13% of The US retail business comes from SNAP," higher than the industry average of 11%, and guided for a 100 basis-point headwind from lower SNAP funding in 2026.
- Headcount and Capabilities: The company will hire additional sales and marketing professionals in third and fourth quarters to address what management described as an overly "lean" commercial organization.
- Debt Reduction Priority: The stated capital allocation priorities remain unchanged, with excess cash focused first on business investment, then on reducing net leverage to the three-times target, before considering other uses.
