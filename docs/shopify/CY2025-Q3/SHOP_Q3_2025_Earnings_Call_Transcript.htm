Takeaways

  • Gross merchandise volume (GMV) -- $92 billion, up 32%, with North America delivering the largest positive variance versus expectations.
  • Revenue -- Up 32% (or 31% constant currency), driven mainly by GMV growth and enhanced by North America outperformance.
  • Merchant solutions revenue -- Rose 38%, fueled primarily by robust GMV and increased Shopify Payments penetration, partially offset by European regional trends with lower Payments penetration.
  • Shopify Payments penetration -- 65% of GMV, with Shop Pay processing $29 billion, up 67%.
  • International GMV -- Gained 41%, marking continued acceleration over Q2 and Q1 figures.
  • B2B GMV -- Increased 98%, with over 155% growth in Canada, and 150% in the home and garden vertical.
  • Offline GMV -- Rose 31% due to increased adoption by retail-focused brands.
  • Subscription solutions revenue -- Advanced 15%, mainly from higher-priced subscription plans and increased platform fee contributions.
  • Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) -- Up 10%, with Plus plans contributing 35%; growth affected by prior-year trial promotions and Plus pricing changes.
  • Gross profit margin -- 48.9%, down from 51.7% a year ago, reflecting mix shift toward merchant solutions and payments.
  • Merchant solutions gross margin -- 38.2% versus 39.7% in Q3 2024, primarily due to PayPal partnership expansion and lower noncash high-margin revenue.
  • Subscription solutions gross margin -- 81.7%, essentially unchanged quarter over quarter, but marginally down year-over-year because of higher hosting and AI costs.
  • Operating expenses -- $1 billion, representing 37% of revenue, and reflecting improving leverage from 45% in 2023, and 39% in 2024.
  • Free cash flow -- $507 million, an 18% margin, slightly exceeding internal outlook.
  • Operating income -- $343 million, or 12% of revenue.
  • Headcount -- Remained flat to down for over two years, supporting improved operating leverage and disciplined expense management.
  • Sidekick AI adoption -- 750,000 shops used Sidekick for the first time, with nearly 100 million cumulative conversations, including 8 million in October alone.
  • Shopify Campaigns adoption -- 9x year-over-year increase in merchant budget commitments; 4x growth in adoption compared to Q3 2024.
  • Partnerships & integrations -- Expanded with OpenAI (ChatGPT), Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity, Klarna, and Global-e, broadening footprint in conversational commerce and payments.
  • Enterprise merchant wins -- Secured new large clients: Estee Lauder, e.l.f. Cosmetics, TWINSET, Welch’s, Formlabs, FanDuel, Ladurée, and Stokke, supporting enterprise migration trend.
  • Fulfillment network expansion -- New collaborations with Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment, Big Blue, DHL networks, Go Bolt, Maple, and national postal carriers increased flexibility for merchants.
  • Cross-border GMV -- Held steady at 15% of total GMV, with no notable change in U.S. inbound or outbound trade post-tariff announcement.
  • Transaction and loan losses -- Totaled 5% of revenue, a rise above historic trend, mainly from payments business testing and higher capital loss rate; trends improving as changes take effect.
  • Cash and marketable securities -- Pro forma $6 billion post-convert settlement; company remains debt-free.
  • Q4 2025 guidance -- Revenue growth projected in the mid to high 20% range, with gross profit growing in the low to mid 20% range, Q4 operating expenses guided at 30%-31% of revenue, and free cash flow margin expected slightly above Q3 levels.

Summary

Shopify (NYSE: SHOP) reported 32% growth in both GMV and revenue, with North America serving as the main driver of upside versus internal expectations. The company maintained an 18% free cash flow margin, while notable operating leverage improvements appeared as operating expenses declined to 37% of revenue. International growth accelerated, most prominently in Europe, and B2B verticals saw near doubling of GMV alongside a strong roster of new enterprise client signings. Advances in AI product adoption and expansion of key strategic partnerships underline Shopify's positioning for anticipated agentic commerce trends.

  • The CFO said, "Our merchants have consistently delivered over 20% GMV growth for 9 consecutive quarters," putting Q3's 32% growth at a post-pandemic high not seen since 2021's elevated period.
  • Shopify Payments' 65% GMV penetration, and Shop Pay's $29 billion volume, were driven by continued global expansion, with Europe’s Payments adoption gains in Q3 exceeding last year's Q3 by over 50%, though international penetration remains below North America.
  • AI-driven shopping behaviors have risen rapidly, with the president stating, "AI-driven traffic to Shopify stores up like 7x," and orders attributed to AI searches "up like 11x" since January.
  • Opex as a percentage of revenue was cut eight percentage points in three years, from 45% in 2023 to 37% in 2025, as headcount remained flat to down, and talent was redeployed to the highest-impact work.
  • The CFO forecasted Q4 revenue and profit growth will face tougher comparables due to prior year’s high-growth quarter and lapped PayPal partnership expansion.

Industry glossary

  • Agentic commerce: Shopper experiences powered by AI agents enabling natural-language, automated discovery, and transaction flows across multiple commerce channels.
  • Shop Pay: Shopify’s accelerated checkout and payment solution offering fast in-app and in-store payments across merchant stores.
  • Sidekick: Shopify’s AI-based intelligent assistant, integrated natively into the commerce platform to automate and support merchant operations and analytics.
  • Gross merchandise volume (GMV): The total dollar value of orders processed through Shopify’s platform in a given period, before deduction of returns and allowances.
  • Shopify Plus: The company’s higher-tier enterprise-grade platform solution targeting larger merchants with more complex requirements.
  • Merchant solutions: Revenue segment including transaction-based services such as payments, shipping, capital, and related integrations, distinct from subscription revenue.
  • MRR (monthly recurring revenue): The sum of fees paid monthly by active merchants for use of Shopify’s platform, measured at period end.
