RISKS

  • US and Canada renewal rates declined by 10 basis points due to lower renewal among new digitally-signed members. "This may still see a slight decline in the overall renewal rate over the next few quarters."
  • Higher healthcare costs outpaced sales growth for the first time this quarter, creating SG&A headwinds. "This was perhaps the first quarter where we've seen health care costs grow at faster pace than our sales."
  • Fiscal 2026 net new warehouse opening guidance revised downward to 28 due to construction delays in Spain.

SUMMARY

Costco Wholesale (COST +0.90%) reported strong top-line growth, with net sales reaching $65.98 billion and a 13.6% increase in net income and EPS excluding discrete tax benefits. Digitally enabled comparable sales rose 20.5%, site traffic increased 24%, and app traffic increased 48% in the quarter. Membership expansion continued, with executive membership upgrades and total paid members both increasing, though renewal rates ticked down as the share of digitally-acquired, lower-renewing members grew. Gross margin improvement was broad-based and supported by core merchandising, ancillary businesses, and the successful rollout of new high-value Kirkland Signature products. Technology investment, including AI deployment in pharmacy inventory, has already produced operational benefits and enhanced member experience.

  • New warehouse ramp-up has accelerated, with fiscal year 2025 openings generating $192 million annualized per warehouse of sales in the year of opening, marking a substantial improvement over prior years.
  • Capital expenditure is on track for approximately $6.5 billion in fiscal 2026, with spending focused on expansion, remodels, depot logistics, and digital initiatives.
  • Nonfood categories—especially gold and jewelry, health and beauty, and special events—maintained double-digit growth, while Gift cards and gold program cycling caused a deceleration in overall nonfood comps to the mid-single digit range.
  • Personalization enhancements online drove a measurable sales lift through more relevant recommendations, and continued web/app improvements supported expanded digital engagement.
  • Management noted retail media as an emerging growth opportunity, with early success in third-party advertising on digital TV and gas pumps, aiding profitability and providing resources to reinvest in member value.
  • Relocations have driven dramatic sales uplifts—sometimes 20%-60%—with ongoing opportunities to upgrade existing warehouses through expansion, increased parking, and enhanced ancillary offerings.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • Kirkland Signature (KS): Costco’s private-label brand, typically providing products at a 15%-20% discount compared to national brands.
  • Ancillary Businesses: Segments operated by Costco including pharmacy, optical, hearing aids, tire centers, food court, and gas stations, distinct from core warehouse merchandise.
  • Executive Membership: Costco’s premium paid membership tier, offering elevated rewards and earlier/extended warehouse access, as referenced in membership upgrade discussions.
  • LIFO: An inventory accounting method (“Last-In, First-Out”) used to determine the cost of goods sold and relevant inventory credits or charges.
