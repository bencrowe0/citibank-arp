Summary

Freeport-McMoRan (FCX +2.14%) concluded fiscal 2025 with adjusted EBITDA of nearly $10 billion, matching prior-year performance and underscoring resilience amid disruptions at Grasberg. The company reported a 5% production increase in the United States, which boosted operating income and validated leach and automation initiatives central to North American expansion. Management clarified that restart activities at Grasberg Block Cave remain on track for the second quarter of fiscal 2026, with 85% of district capacity targeted for restoration by the second half. Over 17 billion pounds of copper reserves were added at El Abra, setting the stage for future asset development and providing optionality for Chilean growth. Freeport-McMoRan maintained strong capital discipline, capping fiscal 2025 expenditures at $3.9 billion—significantly lower than plan—while outlining capital commitments of $4.3 billion–$4.5 billion per year for fiscal 2026–2027, including pre-investment in the Baghdad expansion.

  • Robertson highlighted, "Our balance sheet is solid with investment-grade ratings, solid credit metrics, and flexibility within our debt targets execute on our projects."
  • The company’s leach projects generated more than 200 million pounds in fiscal 2025, with targets of 300 million pounds for fiscal 2026, and scalable growth potential toward 800 million pounds annually by 2030.
  • Quirk stated, "Freeport-McMoRan supplies 70% of the refined copper produced in the US," emphasizing the company's pivotal market share in domestic refined copper.
  • Projected operating cash flows range from $8 billion at $4 per pound to over $14 billion at $6 per pound copper prices for 2027–2028, with management emphasizing high sensitivity to copper pricing in forward periods.
  • Capital discretionary spending for fiscal 2025 was $1.4 billion and is projected at $1.6 billion–$1.7 billion annually for fiscal 2026 and 2027, supporting both LNG and expansion projects in the Americas and Indonesia.

Industry glossary

  • Leach initiative: A process to increase copper recovery from previously mined material by applying chemical additives and heated solutions to stockpiles, aimed at producing refined cathode with low incremental cost.
  • Grasberg Block Cave (GBC): The principal underground mining section at the Grasberg minerals district in Indonesia, representing a major source of future production for Freeport-McMoRan.
  • Deep MLZ: Refers to the Deep Mill Level Zone, a deep underground ore body at the Grasberg site operated on a limited basis in late fiscal 2025.
  • El Abra: A major copper mine in Chile jointly operated with Codelco, now the site of significant reserve increases and future expansion plans.
  • Unit net cash cost: The net cash operating cost per pound of copper after deducting by-product credits; a key measure of mining cost efficiency.
  • Block cave production blocks (PB2, PB3, PB1 S, PB1 C, PB1 N): Discrete sections of the Grasberg Block Cave mine, each with separate development schedules and risk profiles.
  • Autonomous fleet: Mining haul trucks or equipment operated without human drivers, employing automation to enhance efficiency and minimize labor constraints.
