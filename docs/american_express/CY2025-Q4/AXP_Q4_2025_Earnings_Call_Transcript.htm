TAKEAWAYS

  • Revenue -- $72 billion for the full year, up 10%, and a company record.
  • Earnings per Share (EPS) -- $15.38, up 15% excluding gains.
  • Net Card Fees -- $10 billion for the year, up 18%, with Q4 card fees up 16% FX adjusted.
  • Marketing Investment -- $6.3 billion spent in 2025, an increase of around 75% since 2019, with marketing expense up 4% year over year.
  • Technology Spend -- $5 billion annually, up 11% year over year; development and operational enhancements continue.
  • Dividend -- Planned quarterly dividend increase of 16% to $0.95 per share in 2026, with total dividend up over 80% since 2022.
  • Share Repurchases -- $5.3 billion in buybacks for 2025, reducing share count by 7% since 2022.
  • Return on Equity (ROE) -- 34% for the full year.
  • Billed Business -- Total spend up 8% FX adjusted; luxury retail merchant spend up 15%, retail up 10%, restaurant up 9%, international spend up 12% FX adjusted.
  • Millennial & Gen Z Spending -- These cohorts now represent the largest and fastest-growing share of U.S. consumer spending, with average new platinum customer age at 33, and gold at 29.
  • Net Interest Income (NII) -- Up 12% in Q4, continuing to outpace balance growth.
  • Loans & Cardmember Receivables -- Up 7% year over year FX adjusted, with growth expected to continue in line with billed business.
  • Credit Performance -- Delinquency and write-off rates remain flat throughout the year and below 2019 levels.
  • VCE (Value Proposition, Cardmember Engagement) Expense Ratio -- 45% of revenue for the quarter; expected around 44% in 2026 as premium product mix rises.
  • Operating Expenses -- Down 4 points as a percentage of revenue since 2022.
  • High-Yield Savings Accounts -- Balances up 8% year over year; less than 10% of U.S. consumer cardmembers use this product, indicating further growth potential.
  • Platinum Card Engagement -- "uptick of 30% in our travel bookings in the fourth quarter," attributed to the refreshed platinum product and its app.
  • 2026 Guidance -- Management expects revenue growth of 9% 10%, and EPS between $17.30 and $17.90.

SUMMARY

American Express (AXP -0.72%) management announced broad-based double-digit growth across revenue lines and net card fees, supported by record investments in marketing, technology, and product innovation. Guidance for 2026 maintains the company's trajectory for accelerating both revenue and EPS, with explicit expectations for stable credit performance and continued premium product momentum. Initiatives focused on product refreshes, digital engagement, and shifting marketing dollars toward premium, fee-paying segments were highlighted as the principal drivers of results.

  • Chairman Squeri confirmed, "we maintained excellent credit quality" while sustaining high levels of investment and innovation in core offerings.
  • Stephen Squeri reported, "marketing and technology investments are up 20 plus percent," reinforcing company-specific discipline in deploying growth capital.
  • Operating leverage was achieved through reduced operating expenses relative to revenue despite increased technology investment.
  • The proportion of U.S. consumer net card acquisitions paying a fee rose by 8 percentage points, illustrating successful execution of the premium strategy.
  • Management expects net interest income growth to continue outpacing loan and receivable growth, contributing to overall EPS expansion.
  • The company is progressing toward complete migration of data and analytics processes to its third-generation platform by 2027, with early results reducing time for marketing and fraud processes by 90%.
  • High-yield savings account penetration remains low among existing cardmembers, offering a significant future opportunity as premium customer engagement deepens.

INDUSTRY GLOSSARY

  • VCE (Value Proposition, Cardmember Engagement) Ratio: Measures expenses associated with customer value drivers, such as rewards, card benefits, and partnerships, as a percentage of revenue; particularly relevant for assessing profitability in premium card portfolios.
  • FX Adjusted: Refers to adjusting reported financial numbers to neutralize the impact of foreign exchange rate fluctuations, providing clarity on underlying performance.
  • Billed Business: The total dollar amount of transactions charged to American Express cards within a given period.
