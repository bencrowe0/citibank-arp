COUNTER-NARRATIVES & RISKS (Pre-Earnings):

Reasons for Caution:

1. Macro Uncertainty:
   - Tariff regime still evolving, retail spending uncertain
   - Retail advertisers (Pinterest's key demographic) still under pressure
   - Consumer discretionary spending showing signs of weakness

2. Margin Pressure:
   - Analyst commentary increasingly focused on margin compression
   - Operating leverage concerns amid growth slowdown
   - Competition intensifying in visual commerce space

3. Guidance Execution:
   - Low bar set by Q4 2025 guidance cut may be manageable
   - But beat expectations need AI/shopping narrative to gain traction
   - Q2 2026 guidance will be closely watched for momentum signals
