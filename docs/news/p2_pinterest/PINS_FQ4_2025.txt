ANALYST EXPECTATIONS (February 9, 2026):

Wall Street consensus ahead of Pinterest's Q4 2025 earnings call:

- Earnings per share (EPS) forecast: $0.66 per share (+17.9% YoY growth vs Q4 2024 result of $0.56)
- Revenue forecast: $1.33 billion (+15.2% YoY growth)
- Adjusted EBITDA forecast: ~$550 million

Revenue Growth Trajectory:
- Q4 2025 expected to maintain mid-to-high-teens growth rates
- Consistent with full-year 2025 guidance of 14% revenue growth
- Strong advertising demand supporting platform despite macro concerns

PRE-EARNINGS MARKET COMMENTARY (Early February 2026):

Analyst Sentiment on Retail Advertiser Headwinds:

Multiple sources indicated growing concern about retail advertiser spending pressure:

1. Goldman Sachs Analysis (February 2026):
   - Expected revenue performance to be "pressured near-term by macro-related headwinds"
   - Specifically cited potential tariff impacts on retail advertisers
   - However, maintained optimism on long-term growth strategy
   Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/12/pinterest-pins-q4-earnings-report-2025.html

2. Pre-earnings Caution on Tariff Impact:
   - Tariff discussions were emerging in the media ahead of earnings
   - Retail sector facing potential cost pressures from tariff implementation
   - Advertisers in retail sector expected to potentially reduce spending
   - Pinterest as a visual/shopping-focused platform would be particularly exposed
   Source: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/countdown-pinterest-pins-q4-earnings-141507799.html

MARKET SETUP FOR EARNINGS:

Key Themes Going In:

Positive Expectations:
- Double-digit revenue growth maintained
- EPS growth accelerating (17.9% YoY)
- Monthly active users (MAUs) approaching 620 million
- Holiday advertising season potentially strong

Key Risk Factors:
- Macro uncertainty and potential tariff headwinds emerging
- Retail advertiser sentiment unclear
- Competitive pressure from other ad platforms
- FX headwinds from strong US dollar
