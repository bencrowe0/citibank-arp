Report Date: February 6, 2025

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS (January 2025):

According to analyst forecasts heading into Pinterest's Q4 2024 earnings:

- Quarterly earnings per share expected: ~$0.36-0.65 (varying estimates from different firms)
- Quarterly revenue forecast: $1.14-1.15 billion, up 15.4% YoY from $981.26 million in Q4 2023
- This would represent continued strong advertising growth momentum
