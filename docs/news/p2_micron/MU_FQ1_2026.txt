PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST: MICRON TECHNOLOGY (MU)
Q1 FISCAL 2026 (Report Date: December 17, 2025)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS HEADING INTO EARNINGS:

1. CAPEX EXPANSION SIGNALS
   Date: November 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Industry Reports
   Content: Market expectations for Micron to raise FY2026 capital expenditure guidance,
   with analysts anticipating CapEx could reach $18-20 billion to support HBM and DRAM
   capacity expansion. High capex spending signals management confidence in multi-year
   memory demand durability.

2. MEMORY SUPPLY TIGHTNESS CONTINUATION
   Date: November 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Content: Analyst consensus pointed to continued tight DRAM and NAND supply-demand dynamics
   persisting beyond 2026, fueled by ongoing AI data center deployments. Supply constraints
   expected to support pricing power heading into Q1 FY26 earnings.

3. AI DATA CENTER ACCELERATION
   Date: Mid-December 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Market Analysis and Analyst Commentary
   Content: Strong expectations for continued AI data center spending by hyperscalers (AWS,
   Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure). Market anticipated Micron would benefit significantly
   from increased DRAM content per server and HBM demand.

4. HBM PRODUCT MOMENTUM
   Date: December 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Industry Intelligence
   Content: Expectations that Micron's HBM3E and HBM4 products would see accelerating
   adoption. Market monitoring competitive positioning vs. SK Hynix and Samsung in HBM
   supply, with Micron viewed as having strong contractual commitments extending into 2026.

5. GROSS MARGIN TRAJECTORY
   Date: Early December 2025
   Source: Sell-side Analyst Models
   Content: Consensus models projected gross margins in the 55-58% range for Q1 FY26, with
   further expansion potential in subsequent quarters. Margin trajectory improvements driven
   by product mix (higher-margin HBM) and continued pricing strength.

6. RECORD QUARTER EXPECTATIONS
   Date: Week before December 17, 2025 Report
   Source: Pre-earnings guidance consensus
   Content: Multiple analysts revised estimates upward in final week before report, with
   consensus building around record revenue expectations of $12.5+ billion and record EPS
   guidance. Market positioned for potential upside surprise if Micron raised Q2 guidance.

7. INVESTOR SENTIMENT SHIFT
   Date: Mid-December 2025
   Source: Market Commentary
   Content: Investor sentiment shifted more positive with holiday data center build-out news.
   Consensus grew more confident in Micron's ability to sustain strong pricing and volume
   growth into 2026. "Buy now before it's too late" commentary appeared in financial media.

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS:
Strong Buy consensus with target prices reflecting belief in sustained AI-driven memory
super-cycle. Expectations: Q1 would confirm multi-year growth narrative with record
profitability metrics.

KEY CATALYSTS MONITORED:
- NVIDIA Hopper and Ada adoption rates
- Cloud provider CapEx spending announcements
- Competitive HBM supply availability
- Memory pricing trends in secondary market

RISK FACTORS ACKNOWLEDGED:
- Memory price normalization if supply increases faster than expected
- Potential macroeconomic slowdown impacting capex
- Customer diversification (concentration risk with hyperscalers)
- Geopolitical tensions affecting semiconductor supply chain

MARKET PRICING:
Stock had rallied significantly from Q4 lows heading into Q1 earnings announcement.
Options market implied volatility suggested market expected significant earnings surprise
(either direction), creating hedging demand.
