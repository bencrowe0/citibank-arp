PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST: MICRON TECHNOLOGY (MU)
Q2 FISCAL 2026 (Report Date: March 18, 2026)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS HEADING INTO EARNINGS:

1. HBM4 RAMP ACCELERATION
   Date: Early March 2026 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Industry Intelligence Reports
   Content: Market expectations for Micron to announce HBM4 volume shipments with alignment
   to NVIDIA's technology roadmap. HBM4 36-gigabyte 12-high configurations expected to drive
   strong revenue and margin expansion in Q2.

2. SOLD-OUT HBM SUPPLY SIGNAL
   Date: February-March 2026 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Market Commentary and Analyst Reports
   URL: https://tickeron.com/blogs/micron-technology-mu-sold-out-hbm-supply-and-ai-tailwinds-point-to-strong-2026-growth-12100/
   Content: Expectations building that Micron's entire calendar 2026 HBM supply would be
   fully committed under fixed-price contracts. Supply fully sold out before earnings
   announcement signaled extremely tight market and pricing power.

3. EXCEPTIONAL REVENUE GROWTH EXPECTED
   Date: Mid-March 2026
   Source: Sell-side Consensus and Analyst Models
   Content: Consensus expectations pointed to record revenue results exceeding $33 billion
   guidance level. Cloud memory business expected to grow 150%+ YoY driven by AI chip demand
   from hyperscalers. Mobile and client segments also expected to show strong recovery.

4. GROSS MARGIN EXPANSION FORECASTS
   Date: Week before March 18, 2026 Report
   Source: Analyst Commentary
   Content: Market consensus estimated Q2 gross margins reaching 75%+ (record level), driven
   by product mix (higher margin HBM and AI-focused products) and continued memory pricing
   strength. Margin expansion expected to exceed prior guidance of 68%.

5. DATA CENTER CAPACITY CONSTRAINTS
   Date: March 2026 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Industry Analysis
   Content: Market focus on constraints in DRAM supply preventing faster data center build-out.
   Expectations that Micron would highlight DRAM content per server rising and both AI and
   traditional server demand constrained by inadequate memory supply.

6. ANALYST SENTIMENT: HIGHLY BULLISH
   Date: March 2026 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Equity Research Consensus
   Content: 38+ Buy ratings vs. only a handful of Hold ratings from major investment banks.
   Barclays initiated coverage with $675 price target, Stifel at $550. Expectations of
   "memory super-cycle" entering middle innings with 2-3 more years of strong growth ahead.

7. CAPEX IMPLICATIONS
   Date: Early March 2026
   Source: Capital Allocation Discussion
   Content: Market expected Micron to maintain or raise CapEx guidance (targeting $20+
   billion in 2026). High capex spending viewed as necessary to capture market share in
   HBM and meet multi-year customer commitments.

8. CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION RISK MONITORING
   Date: Pre-earnings discussion
   Source: Analyst Commentary
   Content: While generally positive, some analysts noted concentration risk with NVIDIA
   and hyperscaler dependence. However, expectations for Micron to secure long-term supply
   agreements to lock in revenue and margins.

MARKET SENTIMENT: EUPHORIC OPTIMISM
Pre-earnings sentiment reached peak optimism heading into March 18 report. Stock had
rallied significantly post-Q1 earnings, with expectations for continued multi-quarter
beat-and-raise pattern. Market pricing in "secular" AI super-cycle thesis.

AI DATA CENTER BUILD-OUT EXPECTATIONS:
- Server unit growth in low-teens percentage range expected in calendar 2026
- DRAM content per server expected to increase significantly
- Both AI infrastructure and traditional data center demand constrained by supply
- Memory pricing expected to remain elevated through 2026

COMPETITIVE POSITIONING:
- Micron seen as best-positioned among memory suppliers for HBM opportunity
- SK Hynix also strong in HBM but with less capacity availability
- Samsung viewed as slower to ramp HBM4
- Market share expectations favored Micron gaining incremental share

MACRO CONSIDERATIONS:
- No recession concerns factored into expectations (late March 2026)
- Tech sector valuations at elevated levels but justified by earnings growth
- Interest rate expectations stable to declining, supporting tech multiples
- Geopolitical risks (China, Taiwan) noted but not expected to materially impact 2026

GUIDANCE EXPECTATIONS:
Market expected Micron to maintain guidance for record Q3 results with continued strong
quarterly progression. Any guidance raise vs. consensus would be viewed as positive surprise
given already elevated expectations.
