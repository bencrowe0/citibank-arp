PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST: MICRON TECHNOLOGY (MU)
Q4 FISCAL 2025 (Report Date: September 23, 2025)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS HEADING INTO EARNINGS:

1. GUIDANCE RAISE SIGNAL
   Date: August 11, 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Micron Investor Relations Guidance Update
   Content: Micron raised Q4 FY2025 revenue guidance to $11.2 billion (from $10.7 billion),
   non-GAAP gross margin to 44.5%, and non-GAAP EPS to $2.85. This pre-earnings guidance
   raise signaled strong confidence in demand trajectory and memory pricing environment.

2. AI MEMORY DEMAND ANALYSIS
   Date: Early-to-Mid September 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Financial Analysis - AI Bottlenecks End The Commodity Cycle
   URL: https://seekingalpha.com/article/4903810-micron-ai-bottlenecks-end-the-commodity-cycle
   Content: Market expectations centered on AI-driven structural shift in semiconductor
   memory demand. HBM products identified as key growth driver with supply having sold out
   earlier in calendar year 2025.

3. SUPPLY-DEMAND DYNAMICS
   Date: September 2025 (Pre-earnings)
   Source: Market Analysis
   Content: DRAM and NAND supply-demand dynamics characterized as tight across industry,
   expected to persist beyond 2026, fueled by AI data center deployments. Market participants
   anticipated pricing power would remain intact.

4. HBM MARKET EXPANSION
   Date: September 2025 Pre-earnings Analysis
   Source: Industry Research
   Content: HBM market projected to exceed $11 billion in 2025 and potentially reach over
   $35 billion by end of 2025 based on AI infrastructure build-out acceleration. Market
   expectations: Micron positioned to capture significant share given HBM product leadership.

5. MARKET SENTIMENT: PRICING AND MARGIN RECOVERY
   Date: Mid-September 2025
   Source: Sell-side Analyst Consensus
   Content: Consensus expectations pointed to continued memory pricing strength and gross
   margin recovery above 50% (first time since 2018) as AI demand remained robust and supply
   constrained. Memory commodity cycle expectations reset upward given AI tailwinds.

6. FORWARD EXPECTATIONS
   Date: Week before September 23, 2025 Report
   Source: Pre-earnings guidance expectations
   Content: Market expected Micron to maintain or raise Q1 FY26 guidance given continued AI
   data center build-out and contractual commitments from cloud/AI customers. Expectations:
   Revenue to reach record levels with margin expansion continuing.

INVESTOR SENTIMENT:
Pre-earnings sentiment was cautiously optimistic. While Micron had guided higher on August 11,
questions remained about sustainability of AI-driven demand and whether memory pricing would
remain elevated. Some market participants questioned whether upside was already priced into
the stock.

RISK FACTORS CITED:
- Potential memory price stabilization after aggressive upswing
- Execution risk on HBM product ramp
- Macroeconomic uncertainty could pressure data center capex
- Competition from SK Hynix and Samsung in AI memory products

KEY ANALYST TARGETS BEFORE EARNINGS:
Multiple sell-side firms maintained Buy ratings heading into the September 23 report, with
price targets reflecting expectations for continued strength in memory pricing and margin
expansion driven by AI infrastructure demand.
