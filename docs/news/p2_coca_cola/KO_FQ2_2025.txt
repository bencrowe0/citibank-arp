Heading into the July 22, 2025 report, consensus EPS estimate was about $0.92-$0.93 (up from $0.87 YoY); Jefferies modeled $0.94, slightly ahead of consensus. Jefferies also forecast organic sales growth of 3.9%, above the Street estimate of 3.5%. Analysts expected Coca-Cola to reiterate full-year guidance of 4%-5% organic sales growth and 8%-9% EPS growth. Sentiment was highly optimistic heading in: of 25 analysts, 19 rated the stock Strong Buy, 2 Moderate Buy, and 4 Hold - no sell-side bears at all, and coverage explicitly framed the setup as "resilient demand" against a defensive-stock backdrop.
