Pfizer (PFE) - News digest, FQ1 2025 (report date 2025-04-28/29). Pre-earnings estimate context only.

Ahead of Pfizer's Q1 2025 results (announced 29 April 2025), the Zacks Consensus Estimate called for EPS of $0.64 (Wall Street broader consensus around $0.74, later revised down ~1.7% in the 30 days before the print) and revenue of approximately $13.82 billion, implying a modest 0.8% YoY revenue increase but a meaningful EPS decline as post-COVID product normalization continued. Pre-earnings coverage flagged continued Paxlovid/Comirnaty revenue erosion versus the pandemic-era peak, and management's full-year 2025 guidance (revenue $61.0-64.0bn, adjusted EPS $2.80-3.00) as the key context investors would be checking the quarter against.
