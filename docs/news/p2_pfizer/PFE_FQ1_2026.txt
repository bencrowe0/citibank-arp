Pfizer (PFE) - News digest, FQ1 2026 (report date 2026-05-04/05). Pre-earnings estimate context only.

Ahead of Pfizer's Q1 2026 results (announced 5 May 2026), analyst consensus called for revenue of approximately $13.92 billion and adjusted EPS of $0.72 (fiscal-2026 full-year EPS consensus around $2.96, down ~8.1% from FY2025's $3.22). Pre-earnings coverage noted the average analyst price target (~$29.00) sat well above the then-current share price (~$26.38), and flagged continued erosion in Covid-franchise products (Comirnaty, Paxlovid) as an expected headwind, with non-COVID product growth and cost-efficiency progress as the main offsetting watch items.
