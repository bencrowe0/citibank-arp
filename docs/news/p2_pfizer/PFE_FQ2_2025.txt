Pfizer (PFE) - News digest, FQ2 2025 (report date 2025-08-04/05). Pre-earnings estimate context only.

Ahead of Pfizer's Q2 2025 results (announced 5 August 2025), analyst consensus estimates clustered around adjusted EPS of $0.68-0.78 and revenue of roughly $13.56-14.45 billion depending on source (FactSet: $14.40bn/$0.68; Zacks: $14.45bn/$0.68; some Wall Street trackers nearer $13.56bn/$0.78) - a wider-than-usual estimate dispersion heading into the print. Pre-earnings coverage noted Pfizer's ~7% dividend yield was under scrutiny given ongoing cash-flow/leverage questions following its recent oncology M&A activity, alongside continued watch on non-COVID product growth trends.
