Bank of America (BAC) - News coverage digest, FQ1 2025 earnings (report date 2025-04-15)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into its April 15, 2025 report, BAC stock had declined 18.4% year-to-date on fears of a potential recession sparked by tariff wars; banks had been expected to gain from lower corporate taxes and M&A deregulation under the Trump administration, but escalating tariff wars were instead expected to slow the global economy and pressure the banking sector. Analysts expected Q1 2025 EPS of $0.82 (+8% year-over-year) and revenue up more than 4% to $26.97 billion - a marked slowdown versus Q4 2024's 15% revenue growth (which had been driven by investment banking, asset management fees, and stronger trading results). Investors were focused on management commentary regarding how much the tariff wars and macro headwinds would impact M&A activity, trading, investment banking, loan portfolios, and credit quality in the quarters ahead.

Analyst positioning was mixed heading into the print: JPMorgan's Vivek Juneja cut her BAC price target to $43.50 from $49.50 (reiterating Buy), citing tariff-driven risk to investment banking, consumer spending, loan growth, and wealth management, while flagging the possibility of further estimate cuts depending on the ultimate tariff impact. By contrast, Morgan Stanley's Betsy Graseck upgraded BAC from Hold to Buy (lowering her price target to $47 from $56), arguing the post-pullback valuation - 8x her 2026 EPS estimate and 0.8x book value, with an implied 2026 ROE of 11% - was attractive. Options markets were pricing an expected ~9.2% move in either direction on the earnings reaction. Overall Wall Street sentiment remained a Strong Buy consensus (15 Buy, 3 Hold ratings), with an average price target of $50.97 implying about 42% upside from pre-earnings levels.
