KEY PRE-EARNINGS EXPECTATIONS:

Analyst Consensus Estimates (Q4 2025):
- EPS: $0.61 per share (consensus estimate)
- Revenue: $6.44 billion (consensus estimate)
- Performance expectation: Below prior year on both earnings and sales

Market Sentiment:
- Consensus rating: "Hold"
- Wall Street expected year-over-year decline in earnings on lower revenues
- EPS expected down approximately 23% from prior year
- Significant negative organic sales anticipated

Strategic Context (Pre-Announcement):
- Carlos Abrams-Rivera had been CEO through 2025, with strategic initiatives underway
- Business separation discussions had been ongoing in market expectations
- Turnaround efforts focused on margin management and brand investments
- Market cautious about 2026 outlook given persistent consumer softness

Market Context (Pre-Earnings):
- Packaged food sector facing persistent category headwinds
- Consumer spending under pressure from higher SNAP benefit reductions
- Commodity cost inflation moderating but input costs remained elevated
- Retail market share trends in North America continuing to show weakness

Analyst Commentary Themes:
1. Organic sales growth remained elusive despite management efforts
2. SNAP benefit funding reductions creating structural headwinds
3. Consumer trade-down patterns impacting premium/value mix
4. Market share losses in key North American categories continuing
5. Capital allocation decisions critical to investor sentiment (separation, debt reduction, dividends)

Management Transition Context:
- Leadership change from Carlos Abrams-Rivera to Steve Cahillane occurring around this period
- New CEO expected to announce strategic review and possible revisions to prior plans
- Market speculation about business separation proposal and alternative strategies

Pricing Environment:
- Price increases implemented in 2024-2025 facing resistance from cost-conscious consumers
- Promotional environment intensifying as competitors respond
- Balance between price realization and volume loss remaining critical challenge

SUMMARY:
Expectations for Q4 2025 were decidedly negative, with Wall Street braced for significant earnings and revenue declines. The market anticipated that management leadership transition (to Steve Cahillane) would bring strategic announcements, including potential revision or pause of the business separation plan. Investors were focused on whether new CEO would double down on turnaround efforts or chart a different course. Continued SNAP-related headwinds and consumer softness were viewed as the primary revenue drags for the quarter and 2026 outlook.
