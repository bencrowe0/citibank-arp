KEY PRE-EARNINGS EXPECTATIONS:

Analyst Consensus Estimates:
- EPS: $0.78 (consensus estimate)
- Revenue: Expected to decline year-over-year due to organic sales pressures
- Organic Net Sales: Expected to be negative, reflecting volume declines and competitive pressures

Market Sentiment:
- Consensus rating among 19 analysts: "Hold"
- Average 1-year stock price forecast: Approximately 8% below current trading levels
- Key concern: Persistent revenue pressures despite margin improvements

Specific Guidance Expectations:
- Full-year 2025 guidance expected to show modest organic sales growth challenges
- Margin performance expected to remain under pressure
- Investors focused on company's ability to stabilize top-line trends

Market Context (Pre-Earnings):
- Kraft Heinz has beaten EPS estimates in each of its last four quarters
- Consumer staples sector facing softness from economic uncertainty
- Food retail category experiencing volatile consumer behavior
- Inflation moderation expected to help with input costs going forward

Analyst Commentary Themes:
1. Organic sales trends remain a key point of focus
2. Margin expansion efforts being monitored closely
3. Brand investment levels relative to peers questioned by some analysts
4. Market share trends, particularly in North America retail, viewed as critical
5. SNAP program changes creating affordability concerns for lower-income consumers

Timing Considerations:
- Q4 2024 results reported after markets had digested pre-earnings expectations
- Holiday season volume patterns and post-holiday consumer spending in focus
- February 2025 economic data (inflation, consumer confidence) context relevant to guidance

SUMMARY:
Market expectations for Q4 2024 were modest, with investors braced for continued organic sales challenges despite potential margin improvements. The Street was cautiously optimistic about the company's turnaround efforts, but consensus ratings remained neutral due to persistent top-line headwinds. Earnings were expected to show modest beats given historical performance, but revenue misses were anticipated.
