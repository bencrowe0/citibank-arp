Disney (DIS) - News coverage digest, FQ3 fiscal 2025 earnings (report date 2025-08-05)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the August 5, 2025 report, consensus called for adjusted EPS of $1.44 on revenue of $23.75 billion, up modestly from the year-ago $1.39/$23.16B. Coverage centered heavily on the Direct-to-Consumer (streaming) segment, which had grown to over 57% of total revenue but was showing only ~8% YoY growth amid intensifying competition - the key question heading in was whether DTC growth could reaccelerate, and management had already signaled a modest sequential increase in Disney+ subscribers was expected. Sell-side sentiment was constructive: Morgan Stanley raised its price target to $140 from $120 ahead of the print, framing the setup as a "healthy double-digit adjusted EPS growth" trajectory into FY27 if the macro backdrop held, and the broader analyst consensus rating stood at "Moderate Buy."
