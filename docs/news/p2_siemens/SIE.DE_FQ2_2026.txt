SIEMENS AG (SIE.DE) - PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST
Fiscal Q2 2026 (Report Date: May 13, 2026)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS AND PRE-EARNINGS SENTIMENT (as of May 13, 2026):

Key Headlines:
- Q1 FY2026 results released February 12, 2026 showed strong performance with orders up 10% YoY to EUR 21.4 billion and revenue growth of 8%
- Following Q1 beat, Siemens raised FY2026 full-year guidance to EUR 10.70-11.10 EPS pre-PPA (up from EUR 10.40-11.00)
- Record order backlog reached EUR 120 billion after Q1 results, indicating continued strong revenue visibility
- Analyst community maintained constructive to bullish stance with price targets and earnings revisions

EXPECTED Q2 FY2026 METRICS HEADING INTO EARNINGS:
- Analyst consensus expected Q2 orders: EUR 22-24 billion (tracking strong order momentum from H1)
- Revenue expectations: EUR 19-20 billion with comparable growth of 6-8%
- Profit expectations: Industrial business profit margin expected to be sustained at 15%+ levels
- Book-to-bill ratio expected to remain healthy above 1.20

PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT:
- Market sentiment remained highly constructive heading into Q2 earnings, supported by:
  * Continued strong order momentum in first half
  * Successful execution on margin expansion targets
  * Raised FY2026 guidance following Q1 beat and strong backlog growth
  * Positive analyst commentary on AI-related capex cycles and electrification tailwinds
- Siemens stock trading near yearly highs with strong momentum
- Multiple analyst upgrades expected if Q2 met or exceeded expectations

KEY THEMES HEADING INTO Q2 RESULTS:
1. H1 Performance Validation: Q2 results expected to confirm strong first-half trend with mid-single-digit to high-single-digit revenue growth and margin expansion
2. Backlog Sustainability: EUR 120+ billion backlog expected to provide full-year visibility for achieving raised FY2026 guidance
3. Digital Industries Leadership: Continued strength in industrial software and automation expected to drive margin expansion and order growth
4. Smart Infrastructure Momentum: Strong electrification and data center infrastructure demand expected to support segment growth
5. Mobility Segment Stabilization: Market watching for stabilization signs in automotive/rail mobility after prior headwinds
6. AI as Core Revenue Driver: AI-powered solutions revenue expected to be highlighted as increasingly material contributor to order growth

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS:
- Consensus expected Q2 results to support maintenance or slight upside to FY2026 guidance (EUR 10.70-11.10 EPS pre-PPA)
- Potential for modest guidance raises if Q2 backlog conversion or margin performance exceeded expectations
- Digital Industries segment expected to be key focus with potential for mid-teens revenue growth
- Smart Infrastructure margin expansion expected to reach 18%+ range

MARKET POSITIONING:
- Siemens stock trading constructively at elevated valuation multiples (20-22x forward EPS) supported by strong order momentum and backlog
- Option market pricing in moderately high expectations for Q2 earnings beat
- Limited downside risk to stock unless Q2 disappointed on orders or Mobility segment deterioration accelerated

POTENTIAL RISKS/HEADWINDS:
- US tariff impacts beginning to show in costs or outlook revisions
- China market dynamics (K-shaped recovery) potentially impacting growth rates
- Margin pressure if high input cost inflation persisted
- Execution risks on margin expansion targets if order quality or revenue mix shifted
- Currency headwinds (EUR strength) impacting reported results vs. organic growth
