SIEMENS AG (SIE.DE) - PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST
Fiscal Q4 2025 (Report Date: November 13, 2025)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS AND PRE-EARNINGS SENTIMENT (as of November 13, 2025):

Key Headlines:
- Siemens Q3 2025 earnings released in early September showed strong momentum with Q3 orders of EUR 24.7 billion, up 28% year-over-year, and a record book-to-bill ratio of 1.28
- Order backlog reached EUR 117 billion as of Q3 2025, providing strong visibility for growth
- Market expectations ahead of Q4/FY2025 results centered on whether the company could maintain momentum and deliver on ambitious margin and order intake targets
- Siemens raised full-year EPS guidance after Q3 results to EUR 11.20-11.50, up from prior EUR 10.70-11.10 guidance

PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT:
- Stock trading near record highs with shares up 20.44% year-to-date entering Q4 results
- Market consensus expected Siemens to report strong FY2025 results, with particular focus on:
  * Orders in the EUR 87-89 billion range for full year
  * Comparable revenue growth of 5-6%
  * Continued strong margins driven by high-quality orders and digital/automation momentum
  * Record backlog likely to exceed EUR 300 billion
- Analyst focus on whether the company could sustain profitability improvements and capital deployment plans (buyback + dividend)

KEY THEMES HEADING INTO RESULTS:
1. AI and Industrial Automation Tailwinds: Strong demand for automation, electrification, and AI-powered design/engineering continued to drive order intake and backlog growth
2. Siemens Healthineers Deconsolidation: Market pricing in strategic benefits from planned deconsolidation to sharpen focus on core industrial businesses
3. Margin Expansion: Industrial profit margin expected to reach 11%+ on strong orders and favorable portfolio mix
4. Acquisition Integration: Successful integration of Altair (AI-powered design) and Dotmatics (digital technologies) acquisitions completed in 2025

POTENTIAL RISKS/HEADWINDS DISCUSSED:
- Automation market momentum remained somewhat subdued relative to early-cycle expectations, with some indication of market moderation
- Valuation concerns with stock at elevated multiples - limited buffer for disappointment
- Geopolitical uncertainties and potential tariff impacts entering 2026
- Mobility segment continued to face headwinds despite strong automation/infrastructure momentum
