SIEMENS AG (SIE.DE) - PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST
Fiscal Q1 2026 (Report Date: February 12, 2026)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS AND PRE-EARNINGS SENTIMENT (as of February 12, 2026):

Key Headlines:
- FY2025 results released November 13, 2025 showed record annual profit of EUR 10.4 billion and free cash flow of EUR 10.8 billion, with strong guidance for FY2026
- Guidance for FY2026: basic EPS from net income before purchase price allocation (EPS pre-PPA) in range of EUR 10.40-11.00, comparable revenue growth of 6-8%
- Order backlog ended FY2025 at EUR 318.4 billion, supporting continued strong revenue visibility
- Analyst community lifting price targets following strong FY2025 results and optimistic FY2026 guidance

EXPECTED Q1 FY2026 METRICS HEADING INTO EARNINGS:
- Analyst consensus expected Q1 orders: EUR 20-22 billion (drawing from record order momentum in late 2025)
- Revenue expectations: EUR 18-19 billion with comparable growth of 6-8%
- Profit expectations: Industrial business profit margin expected to be sustained at 15%+ levels
- Book-to-bill ratio expected to remain healthy above 1.10

PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT:
- Market sentiment was constructive following strong FY2025 results and analyst repricing upward
- Particular bullish focus on:
  * AI and data-center related demand driving automation and Smart Infrastructure orders
  * Continued strong backlog converting to revenues
  * Margin expansion driven by high-quality order intake
  * Capital deployment plans (dividends and buybacks) supported by strong cash flow
- Analyst price target raises post-FY2025 results, with targets ranging EUR 250-325 per share

KEY THEMES HEADING INTO Q1 RESULTS:
1. AI-Driven Industrial Demand: Strong structural demand for AI-powered automation, data center infrastructure, and electrification expected to drive Q1 orders
2. Partnership Momentum: New strategic partnerships with NVIDIA and Microsoft on industrial AI expected to be highlighted
3. Backlog Conversion: Record EUR 318+ billion backlog providing revenue visibility and supporting FY2026 guidance achievement
4. Margin Resilience: High-quality order mix and digital solutions revenue expected to support industry-leading 15%+ margins
5. Digital Industries Strength: Continued momentum in industrial software (Altair, Mendix, etc.) providing software-like margin profiles

MARKET POSITIONING:
- Siemens stock trading constructively heading into Q1 earnings with analyst sentiment at constructive to bullish
- Valuation at 20-22x forward EPS supporting analyst price targets if guidance reaffirmed
- Limited downside expected unless there are significant surprises on order trends or margins

POTENTIAL RISKS/HEADWINDS:
- Automation market maturation or slowdown if capex cycles soften
- Geopolitical uncertainties (US tariffs, China market dynamics)
- Currency headwinds (EUR strength could impact reported results)
- Execution risks on margin targets in lower-volume periods
