JPMorgan Chase (JPM) - News digest, FQ1 2026 (report date 2026-04-14). Pre-earnings only, via web search.

JPMorgan, Wells Fargo and Citigroup were set to kick off Q1 2026 bank earnings season on Tuesday, April 14, before the opening bell. Consensus called for EPS of roughly $5.45-$5.46 (+7-7.7% year-over-year) on revenue estimates ranging from about $48.6 billion to $49.1 billion depending on the source, with full-year NII guidance previously set near $104.5 billion. The options market was pricing an implied post-earnings move of approximately 3.87% in either direction, notably above JPM's typical historical post-earnings move of 2.71% - a signal that the market saw more uncertainty than usual heading into the print. Coverage framed investment banking and NII as the quarter's dual swing factors: management had guided for investment banking fees to surge mid-to-high teens year-over-year as a backlog of M&A and IPO deals shelved during late-2025 volatility began clearing, while the trading desk was expected to benefit from elevated "AI-driven volatility" (algorithmic trading and sector rotation into technology names keeping market volumes high). The market was also said to be awaiting CEO Jamie Dimon's commentary on geopolitical risk and the full-year economic outlook as a key input alongside the numbers themselves.
