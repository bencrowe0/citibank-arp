JPMorgan Chase (JPM) - News digest, FQ4 2025 (report date 2026-01-13). Pre-earnings only, via web search.

Consensus: EPS $4.85-$5.01, revenue $45.71-$46B. Management had already guided NII to nearly $25B for the quarter (implying ~$95.8B full-year NII). Coverage noted JPM's Q3 beat ($5.07 vs $4.84 est.) had set a high bar heading into Q4, and flagged management signaling that 2026 expenses could run higher than previously expected (technology spend, compensation incentives) - a real cost-side caution flag layered onto otherwise-positive growth estimates.
