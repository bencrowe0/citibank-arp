Ford (F) - News digest, FQ2 2025 (report date 2025-07-30). Pre-earnings estimate context only.

Ahead of Ford's Q2 2025 results (announced 30 July 2025, after market close), the Zacks Consensus Estimate called for EPS of $0.34 and automotive revenue of approximately $41.25 billion - implying a roughly 27.6% year-over-year EPS decline and an 8% revenue decline versus Q2 2024, reflecting the impact of new-model launch costs and tariff-related cost pressure that had been flagged across the sector heading into the print. The EPS estimate had moved up modestly (by 3 cents) in the week before the report.
