PRE-EARNINGS MARKET EXPECTATIONS DIGEST: Oracle Corporation Q1 FY2026
Report Date: September 9, 2025
Digest Period: Pre-earnings expectations and market sentiment (August 2025)

=== ANALYST EXPECTATIONS ===

Wall Street consensus expectations for Oracle Q1 FY2026:
- EPS: $1.47-$1.48 per share (slight growth vs prior year)
- Revenue: $15.0-$15.04 billion (12.8% YoY growth expected)
- Key metric: Total Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) expected to show strong growth driven by AI/cloud momentum

Sources:
https://finance.yahoo.com/news/oracle-orcl-report-q1-results-140002806.html (Published: Pre-earnings August 2025)

=== PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT ===

Oracle heading into Q1 earnings was in a strong position:
- Cloud infrastructure business emerging as major growth driver
- AI workload demand accelerating significantly
- Company positioned as major player in AI infrastructure buildout
- Consensus rating: "Moderate Buy"
- Price targets: $195-$315 per share

The market was particularly focused on:
1. Cloud revenue growth rates (expected strong cloud revenue upside)
2. Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) growth - key measure of future contracted revenue
3. Guidance on cloud infrastructure trajectory through fiscal 2027
4. Signs of major customer wins in AI space (expected)

=== KEY THEMES EXPECTED ===

1. AI Infrastructure Boom: Anticipation that Oracle would announce major contracts with AI/tech leaders
2. Cloud Growth Acceleration: Expectations for OCI (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure) revenue growth to accelerate beyond historical rates
3. RPO Expansion: Market expected significant RPO growth signaling multi-year revenue visibility
4. Guidance: Expected management to raise forward guidance given cloud momentum
