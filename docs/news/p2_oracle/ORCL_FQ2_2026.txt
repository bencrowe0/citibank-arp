PRE-EARNINGS MARKET EXPECTATIONS DIGEST: Oracle Corporation Q2 FY2026
Report Date: December 10, 2025
Digest Period: Pre-earnings expectations and market sentiment (December 1-9, 2025)

=== ANALYST EXPECTATIONS ===

Wall Street consensus expectations for Oracle Q2 FY2026:
- EPS: $1.64 per share (up from $1.47 in prior year Q2)
- Revenue: ~$16.2 billion (approximately 15% YoY growth)
- Cloud revenue: Expected to grow 30%+ driven by AI workload expansion
- Key metric: Total Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO) expected to grow significantly

=== MAJOR PRE-EARNINGS THEMES: GROWTH VS DEBT RISK ===

December 9, 2025 - Two days before earnings, market sentiment was mixed:

GROWTH OPPORTUNITIES (Bullish Case):
- Oracle expected to announce major new AI infrastructure contracts (Meta, NVIDIA expected)
- Cloud infrastructure revenue trajectory could reach management guidance of $18B+ for fiscal year
- RPO growth expected to continue accelerating, now potentially $500B+
- Capacity expansion plans aligned with strong AI workload demand

DEBT CONCERNS (Risk Case):
- Oracle had raised $18 billion in September 2025 bond offering (one of largest tech debt raises on record)
- Leverage concerns emerging: Credit default swaps rising, some reaching highest levels since 2009
- Moody's and S&P issued negative outlooks on Oracle
- Rating agencies warning: Leverage could exceed 4x debt-to-EBITDA by 2027-2028, risking downgrade to non-investment grade
- Question: Does AI infrastructure opportunity justify aggressive debt accumulation?

Market Debate: AI infrastructure growth potential vs. financial risk from rapid debt accumulation

=== KEY METRICS ANALYSTS TRACKING ===

1. GUIDANCE REVISION: Would Oracle raise guidance again given Q1 upside?
2. CLOUD REVENUE TRAJECTORY: Path to $144B cloud revenue by FY30 still on track?
3. RPO GROWTH: Expected to jump another $50-70B from Q1's $455B
4. CAPEX PLANS: Expected clarity on data center build-out costs and timing
5. MAJOR CUSTOMERS: Announcements of new AI infrastructure contracts (Meta, NVIDIA, OpenAI)
6. DEBT STRATEGY: Would management address leverage concerns?

=== FORWARD GUIDANCE FOCUS ===

Particular attention on:
- FY26 OCI (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure) revenue guidance potentially $18B+
- Multi-year cloud revenue roadmap (analyst consensus: $32B FY27, $73B FY28, $114B FY29, $144B FY30)
- Cloud Services Gross Margin trajectory
- Total cloud services revenue growth rates sustainable at 30%+ levels?

=== CONTEXT ===

Q1 FY2026 (September 2025) Results Had Been Highly Positive:
- Exceeded expectations on cloud momentum
- RPO surged 359% YoY to $455B (surprising market positively)
- Major AI customer wins (OpenAI, xAI, Meta, NVIDIA announced)
- Management raised full-year guidance citing AI-driven demand

December 2025 Question: Can Q2 show continued momentum or will concerns about debt/leverage temper enthusiasm?
