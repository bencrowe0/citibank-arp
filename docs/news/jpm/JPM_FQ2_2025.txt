JPMorgan Chase (JPM) - News coverage digest, FQ2 2025 earnings (report date 2025-07-15)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), window: earnings date and following days.

JPMorgan Chase topped analysts' estimates on better-than-expected revenue from fixed income trading and investment banking. The company posted revenues of $44.91 billion for the quarter, surpassing the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 2.52%. EPS surpassed analyst estimates by 17%.

Fixed income trading revenue jumped 14% to $5.7 billion, topping the StreetAccount estimate by roughly $500 million, thanks to activity in currencies, rates and commodities. Investment banking fees rose 7% to $2.5 billion on higher debt underwriting and advisory activity.

Analyst Mike Mayo, who has an overweight rating on the "best-in-class" bank, raised his price target to $325 from $320, pointing out that the bank's second-quarter results showcased internal organic growth continuing to outperform, with 500,000 net new checking accounts and record asset and wealth management revenues.

Fifteen out of 26 analysts are currently bullish on JPMorgan, having strong buy or buy ratings. Nine analysts are neutral with a hold rating.
