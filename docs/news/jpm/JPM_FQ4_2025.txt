JPMorgan Chase (JPM) - News coverage digest, FQ4 2025 earnings (report date 2026-01-13)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance/Zacks). Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Ahead of the January 13, 2026 report, consensus expected JPMorgan EPS of about $4.93, up roughly 2.5% from $4.81 in the year-ago quarter. On net interest income, management's own guidance (reiterated in prior-quarter commentary) called for full-year 2025 NII of about $96 billion, with Q4 NII expected to come in near $25 billion - consistent with the sell-side consensus estimate of about $24.9 billion for the quarter, implying full-year NII of approximately $95.8 billion. Management had also guided to fourth-quarter markets revenue growth in the low-teens percentage range, with consensus modeling equities markets revenue of roughly $2.82 billion, which analysts noted would be a roughly 38% year-over-year jump if achieved.

Beyond the headline numbers, preview coverage framed the release as much about CEO Jamie Dimon's outlook commentary as the Q4 results themselves, given it was the last big-bank print of the season and the first extended read on 2026. Analysts were looking for an early view on how the Federal Reserve's ongoing rate-cut cycle would affect 2026 lending margins and deposit betas, alongside color on JPMorgan's roughly $2 billion in annual AI/technology spending, which Dimon has described as just the "tip of the iceberg" for future efficiency gains. Coverage also noted Dimon's characteristically cautious tone in prior commentary - frequently flagging geopolitical tension or labor-market cooling even during strong results - as a factor that could shape the stock's reaction independent of the headline beat/miss, and separately cited JPMorgan's own Global Market Strategy team's 2026 S&P 500 target of 7,500 with expected market-wide earnings growth of 13-15% over the following two years as part of the broader sentiment backdrop heading into bank earnings season.
