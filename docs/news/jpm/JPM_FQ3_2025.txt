JPMorgan Chase (JPM) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-10-14)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), window: earnings date and following days.

JPMorgan Chase topped analysts' estimates for the third quarter as trading and investment banking generated about $700 million more revenue than expected. Profit jumped 12% to $14.39 billion, or $5.07 per share, from a year earlier. Revenue rose 9% to $47.12 billion, versus $45.4 billion expected.

Corporate & Investment Bank revenue was up 17% year on year to $19.9 billion, while Asset & Wealth Management revenue was up 12% year on year to $6.1 billion.

Despite the better-than-expected results, shares of JPM tumbled 1.9% after the release. Analysts remain optimistic about JPMorgan's prospects; the stock has a consensus "Moderate Buy" rating overall, with 13 out of 27 analysts recommending "Strong Buy," three "Moderate Buy," 10 "Hold," and one "Strong Sell."
