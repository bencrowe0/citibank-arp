Microsoft (MSFT) - News digest, FQ2 2025 (report date 2025-01-29). Pre-earnings estimate context only.

Ahead of Microsoft's fiscal Q2 2025 results (announced 29 January 2025), Wall Street consensus called for EPS of $3.11 and revenue of approximately $68.78 billion. Pre-earnings coverage flagged Azure growth durability as the key swing factor after a run of quarters with cloud growth in the low-to-mid 30%s, plus continued focus on AI capex intensity and its near-term drag on margins heading into the print.
