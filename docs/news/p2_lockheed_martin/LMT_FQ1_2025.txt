Lockheed Martin (LMT) - News coverage digest, FQ1 2025 earnings (report date 2025-04-22)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into its April 22, 2025 report (before market open), Lockheed Martin's setup was defensive after a rough stretch. Shares had declined roughly 7.6% over the trailing 52 weeks, worse than the S&P 500's 1.4% dip though slightly better than the Industrial Select Sector SPDR Fund's (XLI) 7.7% drop. Two recent shocks weighed on sentiment: the stock fell 9.2% on January 28, 2025 after Q4 2024 GAAP EPS of just $2.22 badly missed the $6.62 forecast (adjusted EPS of $7.67 had actually beaten), driven by $1.7 billion of pre-tax losses on classified programs, a 43% plunge in Aeronautics operating profit, and Missiles and Fire Control swinging to an $804 million loss; and the stock fell a further 5.8% on March 21 after Lockheed lost the roughly $20 billion F-47 next-generation fighter contract to Boeing. Compounding the overhang, the Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE) was reported to be probing F-35 program cost overruns, with analysts estimating F-35 margins had compressed about 200 basis points since 2022.

Against that backdrop, Wall Street consensus (per Zacks, via Finviz) called for Q1 2025 EPS of $6.36 (+0.5% y/y, revised up 0.2% over the trailing 30 days) on revenue of $17.76 billion (+3.3% y/y); a separate Barchart preview cited a slightly higher $6.42 EPS consensus (+1.4% y/y). Segment-level estimates pointed to growth in Aeronautics (~$6.98B, +2% y/y), Rotary and Mission Systems (~$4.31B, +5.4% y/y) and Missiles and Fire Control (~$3.20B, +6.9% y/y), against a forecast decline in Space (~$3.22B, -1.5% y/y) tied to reduced Next-Generation OPIR program work. For full-year fiscal 2025, analysts projected EPS of $27.15-$27.79, down from $28.47 in fiscal 2024, with a rebound to roughly $29.6 expected in fiscal 2026. Lockheed had beaten consensus EPS in each of the prior four quarters (including a 16.2% beat in Q4 2024 on an adjusted basis). Analyst positioning going into the print was a "Moderate Buy" consensus (9 Strong Buy, 12 Hold, 1 Strong Sell among 22 analysts), with the stock trading below the average price target of $525.73.
