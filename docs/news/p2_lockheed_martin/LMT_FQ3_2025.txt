Lockheed Martin (LMT) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-10-21)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into its October 21, 2025 report (before market open), Lockheed Martin's setup had improved from the prior quarter's cautious tone: shares were up 5.6% over the trailing month, outpacing the Zacks S&P 500 composite's 0.9% move, with a Zacks Rank #3 (Hold) suggesting the stock was expected to track broadly with the market near-term. Wall Street's consensus (Zacks) called for Q3 2025 EPS of $6.32 (-7.6% y/y), continuing the pattern of expected per-share declines despite revenue growth, on revenue of $18.56 billion (+8.5% y/y) - the EPS estimate had actually been revised up 0.5% over the trailing 30 days, a modestly constructive signal ahead of the print.

Segment-level consensus estimates pointed to broad growth across all four business lines: Aeronautics net sales of ~$6.98 billion (+7.6% y/y, operating profit ~$678.5M vs. $659.0M a year ago); Rotary and Mission Systems net sales of ~$4.74 billion (+8.5% y/y, operating profit ~$474.1M vs. $483.0M); Missiles and Fire Control net sales of ~$3.54 billion (+11.6% y/y, operating profit ~$518.2M vs. $456.0M, the strongest projected margin expansion of the four segments); and Space net sales of ~$3.24 billion (+5.2% y/y, operating profit ~$284.6M vs. $272.0M). This followed a Q2 2025 print that had included $1.8 billion of program-related charges, so investors were watching whether Q3 execution across the legacy/classified programs that had weighed on Q2 would stabilize, alongside continued backlog growth from global defense-spending demand.
