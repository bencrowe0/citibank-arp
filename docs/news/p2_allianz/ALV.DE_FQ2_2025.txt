=== MARKET EXPECTATIONS FOR Q2 2025 QUARTER ===
Pre-earnings chatter focuses on:
1. Premium volume growth: Expecting 5-7% net premium written growth in property/casualty division
2. Investment income: Rising yields supporting net investment income (est. €4.0B H1 vs €3.2B H1 2024)
3. Life insurance headwinds: Continued pressure from low mortality levels and policyholder lapses expected to moderate profitability
4. Guidance sustainability: Market watching whether management maintains FY2025 outlooks given favorable Q1 results and insurance market conditions

Consensus view: Solid print expected with modest beats on investment income, potential slight concern on life/savings segment margin pressure.
