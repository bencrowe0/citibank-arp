Lenovo Group Limited - Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest
Fiscal Quarter: FY2026 Q1 (April-June 2025)
Report Date: August 14, 2025
Digest Compiled: August 13, 2025

=== MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY ===
Ahead of Lenovo's Q1 FY2026 earnings announcement on August 14, 2025, market participants were cautiously optimistic regarding the company's PC and infrastructure business recovery, following a strong rebound in calendar Q2 2025. PC market expectations had shifted from near-term weakness to stabilization, with enterprise refresh cycles expected to provide revenue support.

=== KEY EXPECTATIONS ===

1. PC Market Recovery
- IDC and Gartner had signaled stabilization in global PC shipments in Q2 2025
- Enterprise segment expected to drive growth due to business refresh cycles
- Consumer market segment showing tentative signs of stabilization
Source: IDC Market Intelligence (July 2025), https://www.idc.com/
Source: Gartner PC Market Analysis (July 2025), https://www.gartner.com/

2. Data Center & Infrastructure Growth
- Cloud infrastructure buildout expected to continue benefiting server revenue
- AI-related server demand gaining traction with hyperscalers
- Infrastructure segment positioned as growth driver for FY2026
Source: Mercury Research HPC Market Updates (July 2025)

3. Currency Headwinds
- Chinese Yuan weakness expected to pressure international revenues
- Lenovo highly exposed to foreign exchange fluctuations given HK listing and global operations
- Management guidance discussions expected to address FX impact
Source: Financial Times FX Analysis (July 15, 2025), https://markets.ft.com/

4. Supply Chain Normalization
- Component supply continuing to normalize post-2024 tight conditions
- NAND flash pricing showing weakness, supporting margin recovery expectations
- Logistics costs expected to remain benign
Source: TrendForce Supply Chain Reports (July 2025), https://www.trendforce.com/

5. Management Commentary Expectations
- Guidance on AI infrastructure opportunities to be closely monitored
- Working capital management improvements anticipated
- Operating leverage from volume recovery expected to be highlighted
Source: Broker Research Notes (Early August 2025)

=== ANALYST SENTIMENT BASELINE ===
Consensus expectations showed modest optimism, with most analysts maintaining neutral-to-positive outlooks. The PC market inflection was viewed as key catalyst, while infrastructure segment contributions were expected but not yet fully visible in current guidance.

Compiled from publicly available market research, broker reports, and industry analysis dated before August 14, 2025.
