PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST - MetLife Inc (MET) Q2 2025
Report Date: August 6, 2025

ANALYST CONSENSUS EXPECTATIONS (as of July-August 2025):
- EPS: $2.32 per share
- Revenue: $18.58 billion (expected 8.8% year-over-year growth)

KEY MARKET EXPECTATIONS & NEWS:

1. Premium Growth and Annuity Sales Focus
Sources indicate that premium growth and annuity sales were identified as critical areas for investor scrutiny heading into the Q2 earnings print. Industry reports noted that the North American annuity boom had started to lose momentum, creating uncertainty about whether MetLife's retirement and income solutions business would hold steady or face headwinds during the quarter.

Date: July 2025
Source: https://stockstory.org/us/stocks/nyse/met/news/earnings/metlife-earnings-what-to-look-for-from-met

2. Revenue Growth Recovery Expected
Analysts anticipated MetLife's revenue would reverse the 4.1% year-over-year decline recorded in Q2 2024, with consensus projecting 8.8% growth in the current quarter. This recovery would be driven by volume increases and operational leverage.

Date: July 2025
Source: https://public.com/stocks/met/earnings

3. Analyst Estimate Stability with Profit Revision Caution
While analysts generally reconfirmed their estimates over the 30 days preceding earnings, EPS expectations had declined 4.24% over the prior 60 days, suggesting some tempering of profit expectations heading into the print even as revenue estimates ticked up slightly.

Date: July 2025
Source: https://markets.financialcontent.com/stocks/article/stockstory-2026-8-4-metlife-earnings-what-to-look-for-from-met

4. Major Shareholder Capital Return Program
MetLife announced a $3.0 billion stock buyback plan, signaling board confidence in the stock's intrinsic value and reflecting the company's commitment to returning capital to shareholders amid strong operational performance.

Date: July 2025
Source: https://marketchameleon.com/Overview/MET/Earnings/Earnings-Dates/

MARKET SENTIMENT:
Pre-earnings sentiment was cautiously optimistic, with investors focused on execution in group benefits, premium volume growth, and management's ability to deliver on annuity sales targets amid a cooling industry boom in retirement products.
