PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST - MetLife Inc (MET) Q3 2025
Report Date: November 5, 2025

ANALYST CONSENSUS EXPECTATIONS (as of October-November 2025):
- EPS: $2.32 per share
- Revenue: $18.95 billion (expected 7.6% year-over-year growth)
- Alternative estimate: $2.16 EPS, $18.47 billion revenue (+1.3% YoY)

KEY MARKET EXPECTATIONS & NEWS:

1. Analyst Estimate Consensus for Revenue Recovery
Analysts projected MetLife's Q3 2025 revenue would grow 7.6% year-over-year to $18.95 billion with adjusted earnings expected at $2.32 per share, representing a recovery from mixed performance in Q2. This growth trajectory reflected optimism around volume increases and improved operational momentum heading into the quarter.

A broader range of analyst expectations showed some variation, with certain analysts projecting quarterly earnings of $2.16 per share on revenues of $18.47 billion (representing only 1.3% year-over-year growth), suggesting divergent views on the company's ability to accelerate top-line expansion in the quarter.

MetLife announced preliminary information regarding its variable investment income for the quarter ended September 30, 2025, ahead of the November 5 earnings release. Pre-earnings analysis indicated that investors would scrutinize MetLife's Asia division performance, cost control, and the impact of higher volumes on expense ratios, as these had been identified as key drivers of margin expansion in prior quarters.
