Netflix (NFLX) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-10-21)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), window: earnings date and following days.

Netflix shares were down 9% in morning trading after the company posted a third-quarter earnings miss, with the stock ultimately declining by up to 12% following the announcement. Netflix reported Q3 EPS of $5.87 versus analyst expectations of $6.97, while revenue met expectations at $11.51 billion. The EPS miss was primarily due to a $619 million charge related to a Brazilian Supreme Court ruling on technology transfer taxes.

Analyst reactions were split: Laurent Yoon from Bernstein reiterated a Buy rating with a $1,390 price target, saying Bernstein was puzzled by the negative market reaction. UBS kept its buy rating and lifted its price forecast to $1,495 from $1,450. Morgan Stanley stood by its overweight rating and $1,500 price target. More cautiously, Citi kept a neutral rating and $1,280 price forecast.

Netflix said it posted its best ad sales quarter ever during the period, and achieved record share of TV time in Q3 2025 in both the US and UK.
