Netflix (NFLX) - News coverage digest, FQ4 2025 earnings (report date 2026-01-20)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), window: earnings date and following days.

Netflix reported fourth-quarter 2025 earnings of 56 cents per share, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate by 1.82%, up 30.2% from the year-ago quarter. While the Q4 results were strong, 2026 guidance was seen as slightly softer than street expectations.

There were downward price target revisions across almost all firms covering the stock, including Bernstein and Goldman Sachs, who lowered targets by 8%-10%. The average price target fell 8.2%, with reduced earnings forecasts tied to a lower valuation estimate. KeyBanc downgraded its price target from $139 to $110 (keeping Overweight), citing uncertainty around the Warner Bros. Discovery deal and concerns about lapping 2025's content slate.

Some analysts remained constructive: Guggenheim Partners maintained a buy rating with a price target of $130 (down from $145), noting engagement trends and 2026 profit guidance temper enthusiasm, and that the path to conclusion on the WBD bid will likely keep share appreciation limited over the next three months.

The Warner Bros. Discovery acquisition and softer 2026 guidance were the primary concerns weighing on sentiment.
