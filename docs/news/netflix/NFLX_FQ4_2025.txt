Netflix (NFLX) - News coverage digest, FQ4 2025 earnings (report date 2026-01-20)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Going into the January 20 report, the Zacks consensus estimate (average of nine analysts) was for EPS of $0.55, up about 27.9% year-over-year from $0.43, on revenue of roughly $11.97 billion, implying growth of about 16.8%. Analysts projected a continued, more modest rise in earnings and revenue versus prior quarters, driven mainly by steady international subscriber growth, higher subscription pricing, and further ramp in advertising revenue.

The dominant story overhanging the quarter was Netflix's pending acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery's studio and streaming assets. On December 5, 2025, Netflix and WBD announced a definitive agreement for Netflix to acquire Warner Bros., including its film and television studios and HBO/HBO Max, in a cash-and-stock transaction valued at $27.75 per WBD share (total enterprise value near $82.7 billion, equity value about $72 billion). Coverage through December noted regulatory-path uncertainty for the deal and reported that Netflix had refinanced part of a $59 billion bridge loan tied to the transaction (December 22, 2025). Rival bidder Paramount Skydance's competing, higher hostile offer for WBD was also an active overhang, with investors watching for updates on deal financing and structure as a key swing factor for the print, independent of the underlying quarterly operating results.
