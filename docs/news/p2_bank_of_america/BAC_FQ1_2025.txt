Bank of America (BAC) - News digest, FQ1 2025 (report date 2025-04-15). Pre-earnings only, via web search.

Coverage described BAC heading into results "on a strong footing," supported by resilient net interest income and solid fundamentals. Consensus: ~$1.00 EPS, ~$29.7B revenue. NII guided to grow 7% YoY, a deceleration from Q4's ~10%. Trailing four-quarter beat rate: EPS +5.8% avg, revenue +0.5% avg. Coverage explicitly flagged the rate-driven earnings tailwind as fading going forward - a positive-but-moderating setup, not unambiguously bullish.
