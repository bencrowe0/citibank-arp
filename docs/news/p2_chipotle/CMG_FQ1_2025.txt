PRE-EARNINGS DIGEST: Chipotle Mexican Grill (CMG) - FQ1 2025
Report Date: April 23, 2025
Digest Compiled: April 22, 2025

ANALYST ESTIMATES & CONSENSUS (Pre-Earnings)
- EPS Estimate: $8.24 (consensus from major brokers)
- Revenue Estimate: $2.28B
- Comparable Store Sales Growth: +7.5% (midpoint of ranges)
- Estimate Date Range: April 15-22, 2025

RECENT STOCK PERFORMANCE & GUIDANCE
- Stock Trading Range (Apr 15-22): $2,710-$2,850
- 52-Week High: $2,880 (set in March 2025)
- Analyst consensus rating: Buy (14 Buy, 3 Hold, 1 Underperform)
- Key brokerage price targets range $2,900-$3,100

PRE-EARNINGS SENTIMENT & CHATTER
- Restaurant industry broadly benefiting from consumer dining resilience post-holiday season
- CMG supply chain headwinds easing; previous beef shortage concerns resolved
- Labor cost pressures reported by peers; CMG wage growth tracking sector average
- Inflation impact on commodity costs moderating; cheese and chicken prices stabilizing
- Digital/mobile ordering remains strong growth driver for industry
- CMG innovation pipeline (new menu items) generating positive analyst commentary

MARKET EXPECTATIONS ENTERING PRINT
- Street expects continued traffic growth and pricing power
- Q1 typically strong seasonal period for casual dining
- Analyst consensus expects beat on both EPS and comp-store-sales vs guidance
- Macro sentiment: Consumer discretionary spending holding up despite rate environment
- Recent quarters' track record of beating: 3/4 beats (Q4, Q3, Q2 2024)

KEY RISKS CITED BY ANALYSTS PRE-EARNINGS
- Food safety or operational incident risk (perennial for QSR)
- Commodity inflation spike (though moderated from 2022-2023 peaks)
- Labor organizing efforts at certain locations
- Competition from fast-casual and traditional QSR intensifying
