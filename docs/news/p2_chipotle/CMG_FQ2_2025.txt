PRE-EARNINGS DIGEST: Chipotle Mexican Grill (CMG) - FQ2 2025
Report Date: July 23, 2025
Digest Compiled: July 22, 2025

ANALYST ESTIMATES & CONSENSUS (Pre-Earnings)
- EPS Estimate: $9.45 (consensus from major brokers)
- Revenue Estimate: $2.41B
- Comparable Store Sales Growth: +8.2% (midpoint of ranges)
- Estimate Date Range: July 14-22, 2025

RECENT STOCK PERFORMANCE & GUIDANCE
- Stock Trading Range (Jul 15-22): $2,890-$3,080
- 52-Week High: $3,120 (set in July 2025)
- Analyst consensus rating: Buy (15 Buy, 2 Hold, 1 Underperform)
- Key brokerage price targets range $3,100-$3,400

PRE-EARNINGS SENTIMENT & CHATTER
- Summer seasonal strength driving foot traffic expectations higher
- Menu innovation cycle showing positive customer reception
- Digital channel (online orders, delivery, mobile app) continues strong growth
- Labor market softer; wage inflation moderation benefit highlighted by analysts
- Same-store unit economics improving with operational efficiencies
- Expansion efforts in international markets gaining traction
- Consumer spending holding firm despite higher interest rate environment

MARKET EXPECTATIONS ENTERING PRINT
- Street expects continued acceleration in comp sales growth from Q1
- Pricing strategies gaining acceptance; no demand elasticity concerns
- Analyst consensus expects beat on both EPS and comp-store-sales vs guidance
- Management guidance typically conservative; market pricing in upside
- Recent quarters' track record of beating: Q1 2025 beat both lines

KEY RISKS CITED BY ANALYSTS PRE-EARNINGS
- Macro slowdown in consumer discretionary spending (though not yet evident)
- Commodity cost inflation (avocado prices monitoring point)
- Competitive promotional intensity in QSR segment
- Execution risk on growth initiatives/new market entries
- Social media/operational incident risk
