General Mills Inc (GIS) - Pre-Earnings News Digest
Q3 2026 (Report Date: March 18, 2026)
======================================

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS AND GUIDANCE:
- Consensus EPS estimate: $0.73 (Zacks/BofA range; some sources cited up to $0.75)
- Expected net sales: approximately $4.44 billion
- Organic net sales: expected down 1.5%-2% per revised full-year guidance

RECENT COMPANY ACTIONS & GUIDANCE CUT (all pre-report):
- February 17, 2026: General Mills slashed fiscal 2026 guidance
- BofA lowered FY26 adjusted EPS forecast to $3.44 from $3.70
- Company cited "challenging consumer environment" and weak consumer sentiment
- Revised organic net sales guidance from "down 1% to up 1%" to "down 1.5% to down 2%"
- March 17, 2026: Zacks Research reduced its Q3 EPS estimate ahead of the print

MARKET & CONSUMER CONTEXT:
- Cost of living and housing pressures reshaping spending patterns
- Value-seeking behavior entrenched - "here to stay" per CEO Jeffrey Harmening
- Consumer demand uneven: lower/middle-income shoppers preferring promotions over regular prices
- Stiff competition in protein-centric breakfast products
- GLP-1 drug adoption continuing to drive category shift away from traditional packaged foods

ANALYST SENTIMENT PRE-EARNINGS:
- Stifel maintained Buy but trimmed price target from $44 to $40
- BNP Paribas: Expected reduced guidance due to higher cost of volume recovery
- Analyst consensus cautious but mixed (prior hold/neutral ratings)
- Street research showing reduced outlook sentiment heading into the print

STRATEGIC INITIATIVES:
- "Remarkability" playbook: increased investment in brand competitiveness
- Innovation and product news focus
- Portfolio optimization ongoing
- Blue Buffalo fresh pet food launch planned
- Majority of pricing investment nearing completion
