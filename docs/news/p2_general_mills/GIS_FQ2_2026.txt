General Mills Inc (GIS) - Pre-Earnings News Digest
Q2 2026 (Report Date: December 17, 2025)
===============================================

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS AND GUIDANCE:
- Consensus EPS forecast: $1.02, representing a 27.14% decrease YoY
- Expected net sales: down ~9% YoY to approximately $4.8 billion
- North America Retail (largest division): expected to fall 15% YoY to $2.8 billion
- Key headwind: Yoplait/Go-Gurt divestiture to Lactalis (~6% sales impact from Q2)

STOCK PERFORMANCE & SENTIMENT:
- Morgan Stanley (Dec 22): Lowered price target to $47 from $48, Underweight rating
- Morgan Stanley cut FY27 EPS estimate to $3.65 from $3.80
- Consensus Hold rating from 23 analysts
- Historical performance: GIS beat expectations in three of last four quarters

MARKET CONTEXT & CHALLENGES:
- Weak consumer sentiment amid macroeconomic pressures
- Consumers shifting toward healthier, less processed foods
- Value-seeking behavior accelerating - shoppers buying more on promotion
- GLP-1 weight-loss drug adoption affecting packaged food demand
- Broader packaged food sector under pressure from category shift trends

COMPANY GUIDANCE CONTEXT:
- FY2026 organic net sales guidance: down 1% to up 1% (cautious range)
- Company investing in volume recovery and innovation initiatives
- "Love Made Fresh" launch targeting 5,000 cooler distribution
- Volume pressures require increased promotional activity
