General Mills Inc (GIS) - Pre-Earnings News Digest
Q4 2026 (Report Date: July 1, 2026)

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS AND GUIDANCE:

- GIS beat expectations in 3 of 4 most recent quarters

  - Hold recommendations: 12

RECENT ANALYST ACTIVITY:
