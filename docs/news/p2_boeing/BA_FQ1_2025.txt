Boeing (BA) - News digest, FQ1 2025 (report date 2025-04-23). Pre-earnings only, via web search.

Consensus: loss of $1.44/share expected (vs prior-year -$1.13 loss, i.e. a worse loss expected). Boeing had missed estimates in 3 of the prior 4 quarters, including an 83.2% miss the prior quarter. Full-year 2025 expected loss $2.24/share, with a big rebound to +$2.85/share forecast for FY2026 - i.e. this specific quarter carried low expectations even as the multi-year turnaround narrative stayed intact. Analyst rating: "Moderate Buy" overall, despite the near-term losses.
