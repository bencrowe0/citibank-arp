Boeing (BA) - News digest, FQ2 2025 (report date 2025-07-29). Pre-earnings only, via web search.

Consensus: loss of $1.40/share expected (narrower than -$2.90 YoY), revenue $22.15B (+31% YoY) driven by higher 737/787 deliveries (150 delivered in Q2, 209 cumulative 737s in H1 2025).
