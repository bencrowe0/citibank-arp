Nike (NKE) - News coverage digest, FQ2 fiscal 2025 earnings (report date 2024-12-19)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the December 19, 2024 report, LSEG-surveyed consensus called for adjusted EPS of $0.63 on revenue of $12.13 billion. Unusually for this stretch of Nike's turnaround story, sentiment heading in was notably upbeat - WWD's pre-earnings coverage was headlined "Analysts Are Feeling Good" - with the EPS consensus itself having been revised 1.8% higher over the trailing 30 days, a rare positive-revision signal after several quarters of downward resets. At the same time, coverage flagged that Nike's own prior commentary pointed to gross margins declining 3-3.5 percentage points and sales down low-double-digits for the holiday quarter, so expectations for the quarter being reported were still low in absolute terms even as the revision direction had turned positive.
