Apple (AAPL) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-07-31)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the July 31, 2025 report, analysts expected EPS of $1.42-$1.43 (up slightly from $1.40 YoY) on revenue of $88.92-$89.18 billion, in line with Apple's own guided "low to mid-single-digit" growth range. Gross margin was expected at 45.5%-46.5%, already discounting an estimated $900 million tariff impact for the quarter. Services was expected to lead segment growth with double-digit revenue growth to $26.9-$27.5 billion, while iPhone sales were expected to grow only slightly, helped by promotions and the iPhone 16e. Tariffs were the dominant pre-earnings theme in coverage - framed as an ongoing headwind already baked into the guided margin range rather than a fresh risk.
