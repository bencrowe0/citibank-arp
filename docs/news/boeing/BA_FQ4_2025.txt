Boeing (BA) - News coverage digest, FQ4 2025 earnings (report date 2026-01-27)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (CNBC, Yahoo Finance), window: earnings date and following days.

Boeing brought in $23.9 billion in the last three months of 2025, a 57% increase over the same period in 2024 and topping analysts' expectations. Cash flow of $400 million was roughly double what Wall Street was expecting. The company's airplane deliveries last year were the highest since 2018, helping drive revenue.

It was an incredible quarter for the company with a beat of analysts' EPS and EBITDA estimates. Prior to the earnings, JPMorgan Chase raised its price target on the stock to $245 from $240 and reiterated its "Overweight" rating, driven by a strong outlook for the aerospace sector, supported by large multi-year order backlogs at Boeing and Airbus, rising air travel demand, and an aging global aircraft fleet. Wall Street analysts are highly optimistic about BA's stock, with a "Strong Buy" rating overall.

CEO Kelly Ortberg told CNBC that the company is expecting positive free cash flow between $1 billion and $3 billion for 2026, and that it will "continue to grow as we ramp up production and deal with some of these headwinds that we have to cash flow in the near term."
