Walmart (WMT) - News digest, FQ3 2025 (report date 2025-11-20). Pre-earnings only, via web search.

Consensus: EPS $0.61 (+5.2% YoY), revenue $177.14B (+4.5% YoY), consensus EPS revised up 0.2% in prior 30 days. Coverage flagged tariff-driven cost pressure as the key risk: CEO Doug McMillon had noted retailers were running out of pre-tariff cheaper inventory, with new shipments costing more - a real cost headwind flagged pre-print, not a surprise.
