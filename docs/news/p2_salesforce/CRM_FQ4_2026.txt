Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest: Salesforce Q4 FY2026
Report Date: 2026-02-25
Compiled: January 2026

OVERVIEW
Salesforce approaches Q4 FY2026 earnings on February 25, 2026 with strong momentum from Q3's accelerating AI product adoption, but investor expectations for full-year guidance remain cautious. The quarter represents the company's final FY2026 earnings, making forward FY2027 guidance especially important for stock valuation.

KEY ANALYST EXPECTATIONS
- EPS Consensus: $3.03 per share (9% YoY growth)
- Revenue Consensus: $11.17 billion (11.7% YoY growth)
- Revenue (StreetAccount): $11.17 billion ($11.18 billion slight variant)
- Subscription & Support Revenue: $10.60 billion (+12.2% YoY)
Sources:
- CNBC Q4 earnings preview (January 2026): https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/25/salesforce-crm-q4-earnings-report-2026.html
- Yahoo Finance consensus estimates (January 2026)
- StreetAccount consensus (January 2026)

MOMENTUM BUILDING INTO Q4
Q3 Performance Exceeded Expectations:
- Reported $3.25 adjusted EPS vs. $2.86 consensus (13.6% beat)
- Revenue of $10.26 billion in line with expectations
- Agentforce and Data 360 combined ARR surged to ~$1.4 billion with 114% YoY growth
- Agentforce standalone ARR growth accelerating at 330% YoY
Sources: Q3 earnings results (December 3, 2025)

Strong Momentum Expected in Q4:
- Q4 typically strongest seasonal quarter for enterprise software
- Agentforce adoption accelerating across customer base
- RPO quality improving with multi-year deal commitments
- AI-driven upsell opportunities ramping through account management

FULL-YEAR FY2026 CONTEXT
- FY2026 revenue guidance being raised to $41.45B - $41.55B range
- Operating margins elevated by AI efficiency gains
- Non-GAAP operating margin tracking 34-35% range
Source: Q3 earnings guidance (December 2025)

CRITICAL FY2027 GUIDANCE WATCH
The key focal point for Q4 earnings will be FY2027 guidance:

Consensus expects: 10-11% revenue growth for FY2027, decelerating from FY2026's 10% growth
- This implies revenues in the $45.6B - $46.2B range
- Operating margin assumptions: 35-36% range
- EPS guidance expected: $12.80 - $13.20 range

Sources: Analyst estimates from Simply Wall St, Yahoo Finance (January 2026)

INVESTOR CONCERNS HEADING INTO Q4
- Growth deceleration narrative: Even with AI tailwinds, FY2027 guidance expected to show single-digit acceleration
- Valuation sensitivity: Stock reaction likely tied to whether FY2027 guidance meets or exceeds 11% growth assumptions
- Macro headwinds: Enterprise spending environment remains uncertain in early 2026
- Market share risk: Competitive positioning vs. Microsoft Copilot, Oracle Cloud AI

ANALYST OUTLOOK
Wall Street remains supportive of Salesforce but with cautious tone on growth trajectory:
- AI momentum sufficient to justify current valuation multiples
- But FY2027 guidance will be key to preventing post-earnings selloff
- Any guidance significantly above 11% growth could be catalyst for 10%+ upside
- Guidance below 10% could trigger 5-8% downside reaction

POSITIVE FACTORS
- Strong enterprise customer wins throughout FY2026
- Data 360 and Agentforce attach rates improving
- Customer retention and expansion revenue accelerating
- Margin expansion from AI efficiency gains

DOWNSIDE RISKS
- Macro spending slowdown in Q1 CY2026
- Customer budget delays due to AI transformation planning cycles
- Competitive share loss if Microsoft Copilot penetration accelerates
- Guidance conservatism preventing stock appreciation despite strong results
