Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest: Salesforce Q3 FY2026
Report Date: 2025-12-03
Compiled: November 2025

OVERVIEW
Salesforce enters Q3 FY2026 earnings on December 3, 2025 with moderately optimistic expectations. After delivering Q2 results that beat expectations on EPS ($2.91 vs. $2.78 est.) but saw guidance conservatism, investors are watching for sustained momentum in AI-driven offerings and improved CRPO growth guidance.

KEY ANALYST EXPECTATIONS
- EPS Consensus: $2.86 per share (adjusted basis)
- Revenue Consensus: $10.27 billion (8.8% YoY growth)
- Revenue (alternate estimate): $10.26 billion (8.7% YoY growth)
- Subscription & Support Revenue: $9.72 billion (+9.5% YoY)
Sources:
- Alphastreet pre-earnings outlook (November 2025): https://news.alphastreet.com/salesforce-crm-is-set-to-report-q3-2026-earnings-heres-what-to-expect-
- TipRanks Q3 earnings preview (November 2025)
- Simply Wall St analyst forecasts

MARKET EXPECTATIONS CONTEXT
Expectations for Q3 are characterized as "fairly modest," which analysts note could make it easier for Salesforce to deliver a positive surprise. The company has historical patterns of beating EPS when guidance enters the quarter perceived as conservative.

GROWTH DRIVERS HEADING INTO Q3
AI Products Momentum:
- Data & AI products ($1.2B ARR in Q1, 120% YoY growth)
- Agentforce agentic AI ARR estimated ~$440M in Q1
- Combined Agentforce + Data 360 tracking toward $1.4B+ ARR with 110%+ growth expectations
Sources: Q1/Q2 earnings results and industry commentary

CRPO Guidance Watch:
After Q2 showed modest CRPO growth guidance, investors will be watching closely for:
- Improvement in CRPO growth rates
- Enterprise renewal trends
- RPO quality and duration
Consensus CRPO forecast: ~$30.2 billion (+9% YoY)

ANALYST SENTIMENT
Wall Street remains cautiously bullish heading into Q3:
- Broad "Buy" ratings still dominate
- Main focus on whether Agentforce monetization can drive sustained margin expansion
- Watchlist items: enterprise spending patterns, platform switching risk from competitors

RISKS TO EXPECTATIONS
- Agentforce adoption slower than modeled in consensus forecasts
- Macro spending weakness could suppress enterprise software demand
- Cloud growth deceleration if market saturation increases
- Guidance conservatism could dampen stock reaction despite beat

FORWARD GUIDANCE FOCUS
For FY2027, the street is modeling revenue growth slowdown in the 10-11% range, down from current high-single-digit FY2026 growth. Any guidance that confirms or exceeds this range could drive outsized market reaction.
