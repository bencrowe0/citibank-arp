PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST: Robinhood Q2 2025
Report Date: July 30, 2025
Digest Compiled: July 28, 2025

=== MARKET EXPECTATIONS ===

Analyst Consensus Estimates (as of late July 2025):
- EPS: ~$0.40-0.42 per share
- Revenue: ~$915 million (34-45% YoY growth)
- Growth drivers: Trading volumes, Gold subscriber momentum, Bitstamp acquisition integration

Key Factors Expected to Drive Q2 Results:

1. Trading Activity: Spring volatility from Fed policy uncertainty, market rotation
   - Equity options trading expected elevated
   - Retail participation in market moves

2. Gold Subscriber Growth: Q1 had 3.2M subscribers (87% YoY growth)
   - Continued expansion expected in Q2
   - Revenue per user metrics improving

3. Bitstamp Acquisition: Closed June 2, 2025
   - Expected to contribute revenues but integration timing key
   - Crypto market strength in H2 2025

4. Net Interest Income: Interest rate environment supporting continued NII growth
   - Cash sweep balances building

Market Backdrop (Pre-Q2 Earnings):
- Fed held rates steady in June, market pricing for potential cuts later in 2025
- Crypto market volatility (Bitcoin and altcoin moves)
- Trump administration regulatory policies on fintech moderately constructive
- Retail investor engagement elevated post-Q1 earnings beat
