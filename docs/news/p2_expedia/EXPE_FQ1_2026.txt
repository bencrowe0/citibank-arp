- US travel market conditions expected to improve from late 2025 levels
- April bookings and travel activity expected to normalize after winter seasonality

RECENT GUIDANCE & MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
- Management had guided for strong 2026 with 6-8% bookings growth and 6-9% revenue growth

- Capital return plans (dividends and buybacks) expected to support shareholder returns

KEY THEMES HEADING INTO EARNINGS
- AI product enhancements and scaling impact across business expected to drive efficiency
- Marketing discipline and conversion optimization expected to support revenue quality
- B2B segment growth acceleration expected as key differentiator vs peers
- Pricing power and ADR (Average Daily Rate) growth expected in favorable travel environment

HISTORICAL PERFORMANCE
- Company had consistently beaten consensus estimates in prior four quarters heading into Q1 2026
- Strong operational execution expected to continue
- Market dynamics favorable for booking growth and margin expansion

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY

- Strong EBITDA margin expansion expected (potential 500+ basis points on annual basis)
