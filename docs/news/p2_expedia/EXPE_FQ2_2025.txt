Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest: Expedia Group Inc (EXPE)
Quarter: Q2 2025 (Report Date: August 7, 2025)
Digest Compiled: July 2025

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS AND GUIDANCE OUTLOOK
Sources dated before August 7, 2025 report date only.

CONSENSUS ESTIMATES
- EPS Estimate: Wall Street expected earnings per share of approximately $3.96
- Revenue Estimate: $3.8 billion expected
- Year-over-Year Growth: Revenue growth expected at ~6% (acceleration from Q1's 3% growth)

RECENT ANALYST SENTIMENT & MOMENTUM
- Earnings Surprise Probability (ESP): +7.86% positive
- Zacks Rank: #2 (Buy) rating
- Analyst consensus reconfirmed estimates over prior 30 days
- Strong track record expected: Company had consistently beaten Wall Street EPS estimates in last four quarters

ANALYST PRICE TARGET
- Average analyst price target: $293.71

BUSINESS SEGMENT EXPECTATIONS
- B2B and Advertising growth: Expected double-digit growth continuation (~15-17% expected)
- International revenue: Anticipated strong 13% growth
- Asia performance: Expected strong with ~30% growth in key markets (Rapid API product)
- Room nights: 7% growth expected

MARKET CONTEXT & HEADWINDS
- US travel market expected to remain muted with cautious consumer spending at lower end of market
- Volatility expected to persist in booking patterns
- Margin expansion potential noted as company benefits from marketing discipline and operational leverage

GUIDANCE & FORWARD OUTLOOK
- Q3 guidance expected: Gross bookings growth 5-7%; revenue growth 4-6%
- Full-year guidance: Expected to reflect 3-5% growth in both gross bookings and revenue

KEY THEMES HEADING INTO EARNINGS
- AI scaling impact across business being closely monitored
- Continued focus on B2B expansion and advertising revenue growth
- Strong operational execution in both consumer and B2B segments expected to drive results
- Marketing discipline and pricing power being emphasized by management

EARNINGS PROBABILITY
- High confidence of another earnings beat expected (based on historical track record and analyst sentiment)
- Positive momentum heading into August 7 earnings call
