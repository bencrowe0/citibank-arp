Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest: Expedia Group Inc (EXPE)
Quarter: Q1 2025 (Report Date: May 8, 2025)
Digest Compiled: April 2025

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS AND GUIDANCE OUTLOOK
Sources dated before May 8, 2025 report date only.

CONSENSUS ESTIMATES
- EPS Estimate: Analysts expected earnings per share around $1.38
- Revenue Estimate: Approximately $3.35 billion expected
- Full-Year Guidance: FY2025 revenue forecast increased from $14.3b to $14.6b; EPS estimate increased from $9.33 to $10.83 per share

RECENT ANALYST ACTIVITY & SENTIMENT
- Consensus Rating: "Buy" or "Outperform" across analyst community
- Average 12-month price target: Around $283
- Analyst sentiment was constructive as of late April 2025

GUIDANCE AND MARKET EXPECTATIONS
- Sell-side analysts expected revenue to grow 6.5% over the next 12 months
- Wall Street expected full-year EPS of $17.55 to grow 13.2%
- April was expected to show normalization in cancellations with improved bookings, though volatility expected to persist through Q2
- Easter timing was noted as creating some softness in travel patterns in early spring

KEY BUSINESS THEMES PRE-EARNINGS
- Strong B2B growth expected to continue, with advertising revenue potential
- AI integration across marketing and operations being closely watched
- International markets, particularly Asia, noted as strong growth drivers
- US travel market expected to remain cautious with consumers at lower end of market showing discretionary spending caution

EARNINGS ESP AND MOMENTUM
- Company had consistently beaten Wall Street EPS estimates in recent quarters
- Analysts expected strong execution in both consumer and B2B segments
- Earnings Surprise Probability (ESP) positive as of late April 2025
