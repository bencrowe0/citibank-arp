Data Center gross-profit consensus had actually fallen $1.5 billion from the prior quarter's peak estimate ($29.2B to $27.7B) - a real negative revision heading in, even as overall revenue growth was still expected to be driven by strong cloud-provider GPU demand and continued data-center AI buildout.
