KEY FOCUS AREAS - STELARA BIOSIMILAR COMPETITION

Stelara Patent Cliff Impact:
The major investor concern heading into Q1 earnings centers on Stelara's loss of exclusivity. Amgen launched its first Stelara biosimilar, Wezlana, in January 2025. Analysts project Stelara sales at $1.64 billion for Q1 2025, reflecting significant pressure from biosimilar competition.

Stelara is J&J's second-largest revenue contributor, and the biosimilar launch is expected to be a substantial headwind to earnings.
Source: Morningstar - Johnson & Johnson Earnings Analysis
Published: April 2025
URL: https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/johnson-johnson-earnings-maintaining-fair-value-estimate-despite-heavy-stelara-hit-2025

GROWTH DRIVERS ENTERING Q1

Innovative Medicine Segment:
Despite Stelara headwinds, investors will be watching for growth in J&J's innovative medicine portfolio, particularly:
- New launches and pipeline progress
- Oncology performance
- Impact of the tariff environment

Investor Sentiment:
Pre-earnings sentiment on J&J stock centers on the company's ability to navigate Stelara decline while delivering growth through new products. Market conditions show defensive interest in J&J stock ahead of results.
