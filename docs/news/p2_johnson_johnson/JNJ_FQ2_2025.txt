KEY GROWTH DRIVERS - DARZALEX AND INNOVATIVE MEDICINES

Darzalex Growth Expectations:
Investors are closely watching growth in J&J's key oncology and immunology drugs:
- Darzalex (multiple myeloma): Zacks consensus estimate $4.16 billion
- Tremfya (immunology): Zacks consensus estimate $1.85 billion
- Erleada (prostate cancer): Zacks consensus estimate $1.05 billion

These three drugs are expected to drive Q2 results despite continued Stelara loss of exclusivity headwinds.
