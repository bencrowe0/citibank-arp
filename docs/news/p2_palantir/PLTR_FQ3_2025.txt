Palantir Technologies Inc (PLTR) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-11-03)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets (Yahoo Finance, CNBC, StockStory). Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Palantir was scheduled to report Q3 2025 results on Monday, November 3, after market close. Consensus revenue expectations ranged from $685-700 million, representing continued high-teens year-over-year growth. Adjusted EPS consensus hovered near $0.11-$0.14, with analysts pointing to improved leverage in the business model and potential acceleration in commercial customer expansion. Of 33 analysts tracking the stock, 16 rated "Buy" or "Strong Buy," 15 maintained "Hold," and 2 were "Reduce." The mean price target sat around $38-41, implying 8-15% upside from early-November levels, though sentiment was tempered by broader market volatility and rising interest rates that pressured software valuations broadly.

Street conversation centered on whether Palantir could maintain growth rates in a quarter historically softer for government spending (fiscal year-end budget cycles), and whether the company's pivot toward AI-native products was resonating with enterprise customers—particularly in the newly-launched Artificial Intelligence Platform (AIP) offering. Analysts were also scrutinizing management's confidence in achieving full-year 2025 guidance, which had been raised earlier in the year. A secondary concern was whether the company would maintain operating margins amid competitive pressure in the enterprise data platform space from larger players like Databricks and Palantir's own cloud infrastructure expansion costs. Multiple quarters of stock buyback announcements had also shifted investor focus to capital allocation discipline.
