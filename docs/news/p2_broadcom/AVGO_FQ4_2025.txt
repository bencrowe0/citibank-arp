ANALYST EXPECTATIONS GOING IN:

- Consensus revenue estimate: $17.45-17.49B

PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT:
- AI infrastructure buildout continuing to accelerate
- Custom AI accelerator demand from major hyperscalers viewed as sustainable
- Broadcom stock trading near all-time highs leading into earnings
- Options market pricing for modest volatility (earnings not viewed as binary risk)
- AI semiconductor segment expected to represent ~45-50% of total revenue

ANALYST COMMENTARY PRE-EARNINGS:
- Broadcom positioned as the primary infrastructure semiconductor winner of 2025
- AI-driven growth narrative increasingly mature and well-understood by market
- Networking software/infrastructure components gaining analyst attention as growth lever
- Concern about near-term growth sustainability post-Q4 given law of large numbers
- Multiple analyst reports flagging 2026 guidance as key focus point

KEY UNKNOWNS AHEAD OF CALL:
- Absolute magnitude of AI revenue in Q4 (would set tone for FY2026 growth rates)
- Management's 2026 AI revenue outlook given exponential run-rate in 2025
- Whether custom AI accelerator demand would continue accelerating or normalize
- Impact of networking segment growth on overall gross margins
- Guidance for Q1 2026 (would be first "deceleration" test point)

Market expectations: Strong beat expected with AI revenue accelerating to $8B+ run-rate, Q1 2026 guidance critical for forward valuation.
