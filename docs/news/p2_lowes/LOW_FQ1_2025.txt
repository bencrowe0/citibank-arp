Lowe's (LOW) - News coverage digest, FQ1 2025 earnings (report date 2025-05-21)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the May 21, 2025 report, consensus estimates centered on EPS of about $2.89-$2.90 (down from $3.06-$3.08 YoY) on revenue of $20.95-$21 billion (down ~1-2% YoY) - a rare pre-earnings setup where the Street was modeling a YoY decline rather than growth. Lowe's own outlook called the 2025 home-improvement market roughly flat, with the Pro segment expected to outpace DIY, driven by repair/maintenance demand; comparable sales were guided flat to up 1% for the year, with some spring demand expected to shift from Q1 into Q2 due to weather.
