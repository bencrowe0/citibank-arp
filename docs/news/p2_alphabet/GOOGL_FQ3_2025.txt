Alphabet Q3 2025 Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest

REPORT DATE: 2025-10-29
DIGEST COMPILED: Pre-earnings market outlook articles from before October 29, 2025

=== MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY ===

Wall Street was anticipating a major milestone heading into Alphabet's Q3 2025 earnings report - the potential for the company's first-ever $100 billion quarterly revenue quarter. The earnings call was scheduled for October 29, 2025, after market close.

KEY PRE-EARNINGS EXPECTATIONS:
- Total Revenue: $99.75-$99.90 billion (market watch for $100B symbolic milestone)
- Earnings Per Share: $2.29 expected
- Market expected double-digit growth across all segments

SEGMENT OUTLOOK:
- Search: Strong retail and vertical strength expected
- YouTube: Double-digit advertising growth anticipated  
- Google Cloud: Accelerating growth expected, driven by AI and enterprise demand
- Capital Expenditure: Market focused on effectiveness of massive CapEx investments in AI infrastructure

MAJOR INVESTOR FOCUS AREAS:
1. Achievement of $100 billion revenue milestone - symbolic and practical achievement
2. Capital Expenditure efficiency - whether massive AI infrastructure spending was yielding returns
3. Google Cloud acceleration and margin expansion
4. AI monetization progress and competitive positioning

ANALYST SENTIMENT:
Markets expected high volatility around the announcement - large potential stock movement of ±6% was anticipated.
