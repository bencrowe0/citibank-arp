Alphabet Q1 2026 Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest

REPORT DATE: 2026-04-29
DIGEST COMPILED: Pre-earnings market outlook articles from before April 29, 2026

=== MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY ===

Heading into Alphabet's Q1 2026 earnings report scheduled for April 29, 2026, Wall Street was expecting strong results driven by continued Google Cloud acceleration and AI monetization progress. The earnings call was anticipated after market close on April 29, 2026.

KEY PRE-EARNINGS EXPECTATIONS:
- Total Revenue: $106.9 billion (+19% YoY)
- Earnings Per Share: $2.63-2.73 (consensus: $2.73 with range from $2.46-$2.73)
- Operating margin expected to improve

SEGMENT EXPECTATIONS:
- Search Revenue: Approximately 15.9% YoY growth expected
- Google Cloud Revenue: Approximately $18.4 billion expected
  * Growth rate: 49-49.6% YoY expected
  * Margin expansion: Expected to continue (up from 20% in October 2025 to 27%)
  * AI solutions: Driving significant revenue acceleration
- YouTube: Continued advertising growth

MAJOR ANALYST WATCHPOINTS:
1. Google Cloud growth rate vs. Q4 benchmark (previous quarter achieved 48% growth)
2. Search revenue resilience under AI Overview integration
3. Generative AI monetization progress - particularly through Gemini integration
4. Management commentary on 2026 CapEx guidance sustainability
5. Cloud backlog trends and enterprise AI demand signals

CAPITAL EXPENDITURE CONTEXT:
- Q1 CapEx expected: $35.7 billion
- 2026 full-year guidance: $175-185 billion (confirmed)
- 2027 CapEx: Expected to significantly exceed 2026 levels

ANALYST COMMENTARY:
- Consensus Buy rating from broad analyst coverage (50% Strong Buy, 38% Buy, 13% Hold per public data)
- Average 12-month price target: $351-354
- Price target range: $220-$420
- Focus: Cloud acceleration driving margin expansion and EPS upside

KEY ESTIMATES REVISION TRENDS:
- Since October 2025, Google Cloud margin expanded from 20% to 27%, driving EPS expectations UP from $2.46 to $2.73
- Depreciation acceleration from AI infrastructure investment expected to pressure EPS growth YoY (~-6.4% EPS decline YoY)
