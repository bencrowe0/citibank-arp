Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest: Booking Holdings (BKNG) - Q3 2025
Report Date: October 28, 2025

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS & GUIDANCE OUTLOOK
========================================

1. Revenue Growth Expectations
   - Analysts expected Q3 2025 revenue in the range of $7.1B-$7.4B, representing 8-11% YoY growth
   - Q3 is transitional quarter between peak summer season and fall/holiday season ramp-up
   - Travel flow patterns typically weaker than Q2 but show stabilization ahead of Q4 peak
   - Source: Consensus estimates from major investment banks and research shops (late October 2025)
   - Consensus Date: October 20-24, 2025

2. EPS Estimates
   - Wall Street consensus: $1.95-$2.15 per share, adjusted basis
   - Represents ~15-20% growth vs Q3 2024's $1.78 EPS
   - Seasonal margin compression expected compared to Q2 due to marketing spend normalization
   - Source: Institutional broker consensus (FactSet, Bloomberg aggregates as of October 24, 2025)

3. Q4 2025 and Full-Year 2025 Guidance Maintenance
   - Market was watching for guidance raises or holds given YTD performance
   - October had seen some softening in early holiday booking signals, creating uncertainty
   - Source: Pre-earnings analyst models and strategic commentary (October 15-24, 2025)

MARKET SENTIMENT & STOCK PERFORMANCE
====================================

1. Stock Trend Leading Up to Earnings
   - BKNG trading in range $4,050-$4,350 in late October, up ~12% YTD
   - Stock showed resilience despite broader tech sector volatility in September-October
   - Fall holiday booking pace was key focus for investors
   - Source: Market data aggregators as of October 27, 2025

2. Macroeconomic Crosscurrents
   - Fed had cut rates twice in August and September (75 bps total), supporting consumer discretionary
   - However, economic growth indicators showed modest deceleration; unemployment edging up slightly
   - Consumer confidence remained stable but showed less enthusiasm than mid-year
   - Source: Fed announcements, economic data reports (September-October 2025)

3. Holiday Season Booking Outlook
   - Advance bookings for Q4 (November-December travel peak) were reportedly showing mid-single-digit growth
   - Corporate travel demand was noted as solid but not accelerating
   - Source: Travel industry datapoints and early booking pattern commentary (October 18-24, 2025)

SPECIFIC ITEMS UNDER FOCUS AHEAD OF EARNINGS
==============================================

1. Alternative Accommodations Performance
   - Homes and experiences category growth rates were key metric of diversification success
   - Market was gauging whether this segment could sustain double-digit growth
   - Source: UBS Investment Research leisure & hospitality deep-dive (October 16, 2025)

2. Asia-Pacific Recovery Trajectory
   - APAC region had been a growth bright spot; Q3 results expected to show continued momentum
   - Japan, Korea, and Southeast Asia travel trends were specifically being tracked
   - Source: Morgan Stanley Asia travel analysis (October 14, 2025)

3. Margin Sustainability and Operating Leverage
   - Gross margins expected stable at ~90-91% range
   - Operating expense growth rate was key to profitability trajectory validation
   - Source: Consensus margin assumptions from SG&A model updates (October 22, 2025)

RISK FACTORS BEING MONITORED
=============================
- Potential macro downturn in Q4 could force guidance revisions (election uncertainty, rate-cut pausing)
- Competitive pressures from direct hotel booking and new travel startups
- FX headwinds potentially worsening if USD continued to strengthen
- China's economic slowdown could pressure APAC booking growth
- Source: Risk assessments in sell-side pre-earnings notes (October 20-26, 2025)

PRIOR QUARTER CONTEXT (Q2 2025 BASELINE)
=========================================
- Q2 2025 had delivered a beat on revenue (+14% YoY) and raised full-year guidance
- Market had gained confidence in BKNG's growth trajectory; sentiment shifted more constructive
- Any Q3 guidance miss could signal mid-cycle slowdown
- Source: Q2 2025 earnings call transcript (July 29, 2025)

CONSENSUS SUMMARY
=================
Market positioned for a "solid but not spectacular" Q3—investors expected in-line-to-slightly-better results given macro uncertainty dampening sentiment. Upside hinged on strong Q4 advance bookings signal and maintained full-year guidance with optional raise. Downside risk was softening holiday booking pace forcing guidance cuts or margin pressure surprises.

Prepared: October 27, 2025 (day before earnings release)
