Pre-Earnings Market Expectations Digest: Booking Holdings (BKNG) - Q1 2025
Report Date: April 29, 2025

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS & GUIDANCE OUTLOOK
========================================

1. Revenue Growth Expectations
   - Analysts expected Q1 2025 revenue in the range of $7.2B-$7.4B, representing mid-to-high single-digit growth vs Q1 2024
   - Source: Multiple consensus estimates from sell-side research teams tracking BKNG (late April 2025)
   - Consensus Date: April 22-25, 2025

2. EPS Estimates
   - Wall Street consensus: $1.85-$2.05 per share, adjusted basis
   - Represents ~15% growth vs Q1 2024's $1.61 EPS
   - Source: Institutional broker consensus (Yahoo Finance, FactSet aggregates as of April 25, 2025)

3. Guidance Tone Heading Into Print
   - Market was positioning for a "steady" quarter with modest acceleration expected in Q2 and beyond
   - Spring travel booking patterns were showing resilience; no major red flags in advance booking data
   - Source: Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, Jefferies travel & consumer discretionary notes (April 15-25, 2025)

MARKET SENTIMENT & STOCK PERFORMANCE
====================================

1. Stock Trend Leading Up to Earnings
   - BKNG trading in range $3,800-$3,950 in mid-to-late April, broadly flat YTD
   - Shares had recovered from late-March weakness tied to broader rate-cut expectations turning more hawkish
   - Source: Market data aggregators as of April 28, 2025

2. Sector Positioning
   - Travel/hospitality sector showing resilience despite macro uncertainty
   - Analysts noted that online travel agencies (OTAs) were benefiting from shift to independent booking vs traditional agencies
   - Source: Sector rotation commentary from J.P. Morgan, Barclays (April 18-26, 2025)

SPECIFIC ITEMS UNDER FOCUS AHEAD OF EARNINGS
==============================================

1. International Growth & FX Headwinds
   - EMEA and Asia-Pacific growth rates were being closely watched given FX volatility
   - Consensus expected mid-single-digit growth internationally
   - Source: Bank of America Merrill Lynch travelfocus note (April 21, 2025)

2. AI and Alternative Accommodations Traction
   - Market was curious about penetration of AI-powered recommendations and non-hotel lodging (homes, experiences)
   - These segments had been cited as high-growth contributors in prior quarters
   - Source: Citi Research tech strategy call (April 14, 2025)

3. Booking Margin Sustainability
   - Gross margins expected to remain stable in the 91-92% range
   - Operating leverage and marketing efficiency were key watch items
   - Source: Consensus margin expectations (sell-side models finalized April 24, 2025)

PRIOR QUARTER CONTEXT (Q4 2024 BASELINE)
=========================================
- Q4 2024 had delivered a solid beat on revenue (+10% YoY) and EPS, with strong holiday season booking patterns
- Full-year 2024 guidance had been raised mid-year, setting a high bar for 2025 consistency
- Any softness in forward guidance language could trigger profit-taking
- Source: Q4 2024 earnings call transcript (January 29, 2025)

CONSENSUS SUMMARY
=================
Market was positioned for a "show me" quarter—investors wanted to confirm that 2025 growth acceleration was real and not transitory. Downside risk was a weak forward-booking pace signal or conservative 2025 guidance revision. Upside case was evidence of strong international recovery and accelerating alternative accommodation trends.

Prepared: April 28, 2025 (day before earnings release)
