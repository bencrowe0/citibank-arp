PepsiCo (PEP) - News coverage digest, FQ4 2024 earnings (report date 2025-02-04)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the February 4, 2025 report, the Zacks Consensus Estimate called for Q4 revenue of about $27.9 billion (+0.3% year-over-year) and EPS of $1.95 (+9.6% from $1.78 a year earlier). Despite the EPS growth implied by consensus, pre-earnings sentiment was explicitly bearish: PepsiCo carried a Zacks Rank #4 (Sell) with a negative Earnings ESP of -0.43% heading into the print, and coverage cited "muted estimate revisions and a bearish outlook" as reasons for a cautious stance, explicitly recommending investors "wait for clearer signs of recovery" rather than buy ahead of the report.

The negative setup was attributed to persistent inflationary pressure, operational challenges in North America (including the lingering fallout from Quaker Foods North America product recalls), and shifting/weakening consumer behavior that had been hurting performance through 2024. This left PepsiCo heading into its Q4 print with unusually depressed sentiment for a large-cap consumer staple - a Sell rating and negative revisions bias rather than the constructive-to-neutral tone seen ahead of most of its 2023 prints.
