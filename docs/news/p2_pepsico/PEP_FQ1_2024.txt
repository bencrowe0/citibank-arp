PepsiCo (PEP) - News coverage digest, FQ1 2024 earnings (report date 2024-04-23)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the April 23, 2024 report, consensus called for Q1 revenue of about $18.1 billion and adjusted EPS of $1.52, with Trefis modeling slightly ahead at $18.2 billion / $1.55, crediting continued benefit from pricing initiatives (particularly on smaller pack sizes) even as North America volumes stayed pressured by product recalls and softer demand among lower-income consumers. The pre-earnings framing was mildly constructive - "will PepsiCo beat the consensus" - rather than confidently bullish, reflecting the deceleration already visible in the prior (Q4 2023) print.

The stock's multi-year setup going into the quarter was one of notable relative underperformance: PEP shares were trading around $170, up only about 15% since early 2021 versus roughly 35% for the S&P 500 over the same stretch, with annual returns of +17% (2021), +4% (2022), and -6% (2023) against the S&P's +27%/-19%/+24% - i.e., PEP had lagged the broader market badly in the strong 2023 rally. This left the stock trading at a depressed relative valuation heading into the print, with bulls framing it as room for a re-rating if pricing power held up.
