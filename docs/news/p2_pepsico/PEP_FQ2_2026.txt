PepsiCo (PEP) - News coverage digest, FQ2 2026 earnings (report date 2026-07-09)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the July 9, 2026 report (before market open), consensus among roughly 16 covering analysts called for Q2 EPS of about $2.19-$2.21 (estimate range $2.14-$2.55) on revenue near $23.96-24.0 billion. Estimates had drifted lower into the print - the EPS consensus was down about 1.3% over the trailing 30 days and 2.6% over the trailing 90 days from where it stood a quarter earlier - and the stock itself had fallen roughly 11% heading into the report, trading around a closely-watched $144 support level. Multiple sell-side desks trimmed price targets ahead of the print: UBS flagged PepsiCo as one of its worst-performing consumer-staples names since April, while Barclays and Bernstein both cited continued market-share pressure in snacks and beverages. JPMorgan was a notable holdout, maintaining its Overweight rating despite also cutting its target, arguing that PepsiCo's consistent earnings track record and already-low expectations left room for a positive surprise even on a modest beat. Shares were quoted around 16.3x forward earnings ahead of the report - below both the S&P 500 and consumer-staples peers - with commentary noting the depressed valuation, high dividend yield, and robust free cash flow as an offsetting, more constructive strand of pre-earnings sentiment.

Options traders were pricing a relatively contained implied post-earnings move of about 4.1-4.5% in either direction, well below PepsiCo's average post-earnings move of roughly 7.4% over the prior four quarters - consistent with the "low bar" framing that dominated pre-earnings coverage. Organic revenue growth and core constant-currency EPS trends were flagged as the key metrics to watch, along with the first detailed read on demand trends through mid-June amid moderating consumer spending.
