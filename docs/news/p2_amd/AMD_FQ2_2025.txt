AMD - News coverage digest, FQ2 2025 earnings (report date 2025-08-05)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the August 5, 2025 report, analysts expected Q2 EPS of $0.48 (down ~30% YoY, largely reflecting export-license-related charges) on revenue up 27% YoY to $7.41 billion. Coverage noted AMD had beaten consensus soundly the prior quarter (Q1: $7.44B revenue +36% YoY, non-GAAP EPS $0.96 +55% YoY, beating consensus by ~4.5%/2.8%), with the data-center segment ($3.7B, +57% YoY) driving that beat via EPYC Turin CPU and Instinct GPU adoption. Sentiment heading into Q2 was cautiously bullish: several analysts remained positive despite margin-pressure concerns tied to export-license impacts, with the market's attention on fresh guidance for revenue, margins, and the trajectory of hyperscaler data-center demand rather than the EPS headline itself (which was already expected to look weak YoY due to the known charge).
