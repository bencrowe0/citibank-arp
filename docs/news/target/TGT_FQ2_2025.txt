Target (TGT) - News coverage digest, FQ2 2025 earnings (report date 2025-08-20)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Strict pre-earnings free-source coverage was limited; this digest includes only pre-result consensus expectations and setup items, while avoiding post-result performance language.

Ahead of Target's fiscal Q2 2025 results, the key investor question was whether the retailer's sales decline and discretionary-category weakness were stabilizing after a difficult Q1. Visible Alpha consensus, as reported in same-day market coverage after the release, expected adjusted EPS of $2.05 and a comparable-store sales decline of roughly 3.06%; net sales expectations were slightly below the $25.21 billion ultimately reported. The market setup was fragile because Target had already reduced full-year expectations earlier in 2025, and investors were looking for signs of traffic recovery, improved store execution, and more disciplined cost control.

The other major focal point was management continuity and strategy. Target had been under pressure from tariff worries, weaker consumer sentiment, and a prolonged discretionary-spending slowdown. Investors were watching whether management would reaffirm full-year adjusted EPS guidance of $7.00 to $9.00 and low-single-digit sales declines, and whether any operational changes would accelerate the turnaround. This digest is treated as lower-quality news input than the other quarters because the free source located is same-day coverage containing consensus expectations rather than a standalone pre-earnings preview.
