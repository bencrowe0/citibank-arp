Target (TGT) - News coverage digest, FQ1 2025 earnings (report date 2025-05-21)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Ahead of Target's fiscal Q1 2025 results before market open on Wednesday, analyst sentiment was cautious rather than bullish. Of 13 analysts tracked by Visible Alpha, eight rated TGT a Hold and five rated it a Buy. The stock had already fallen more than 25% year-to-date, while the average price target of $117.54 still implied nearly 20% upside from the prior Friday close. Consensus expected EPS of $1.64 on revenue of $24.41 billion, with comparable store sales down 1.5% year-over-year; some analysts warned the comparable-sales decline could be closer to 4%.

The main setup was whether Target would cut full-year guidance after warning last quarter that tariffs and worsening consumer sentiment were likely to pressure future performance. JPMorgan expected lower full-year sales and profit forecasts but remained constructive on Target's long-term relevance. Morgan Stanley also expected guidance to come down, citing weaker consumer sentiment, discretionary-category softness, and Target-specific execution problems. Oppenheimer remained more positive on long-term upside, while UBS framed the key issues as stabilizing market share, defining the brand's market position, and demonstrating sustainable profitability.
