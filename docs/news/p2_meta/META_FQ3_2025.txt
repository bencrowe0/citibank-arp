Meta (META) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-10-29)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Heading into the October 29, 2025 report, analysts expected revenue of $49.4 billion (+21.6% YoY) and EPS of $6.74 (+11.8% YoY), with net income forecast at $17.0 billion (+8.5% YoY) and operating margin expected at 38.7% (down from 42.7% a year earlier - margin compression already priced into the consensus). Coverage centered on AI-monetization progress and ROI concerns around Meta's heavy AI/infrastructure capex - the dominant pre-earnings theme was whether ad-revenue growth could justify the spending pace, not whether the core ad business itself would grow.
