Walt Disney (DIS) - News coverage digest, FQ3 2025 earnings (report date 2025-08-06)
Fetched via web search across free financial outlets. Window: pre-earnings coverage strictly before report_date, no post-earnings content.

Ahead of Disney's fiscal Q3 2025 results before market open on Wednesday, analysts tracked by Visible Alpha were broadly bullish: six of seven rated DIS a Buy, with one Hold, and the average price target was near $136, implying roughly 17% upside from the prior Friday close. Consensus expected revenue of $23.75 billion and adjusted EPS of $1.48, both above the year-earlier quarter. The setup was constructive because Disney had beaten estimates in Q2, reported an unexpected Disney+ subscriber increase, lifted full-year adjusted EPS guidance to $5.75, and announced plans for a new Abu Dhabi theme park.

Investor focus was on whether Experiences demand remained resilient despite macro and Epic Universe competition, whether direct-to-consumer margins kept improving, and whether Disney could maintain a stronger narrative into FY2026. UBS raised its price target to $138 from $120, citing resilient parks demand and streaming profitability ahead of the ESPN standalone streaming launch. Jefferies had recently upgraded Disney to Buy with a $144 target, pointing to upcoming film/streaming releases and two new cruise ships by year-end as catalysts. The market context was moderately positive: DIS had gained about 8% year-to-date before the print.
