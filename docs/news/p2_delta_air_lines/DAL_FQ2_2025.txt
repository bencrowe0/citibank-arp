Delta Air Lines - Pre-Earnings News Digest: Q2 2025 (Report Date: July 10, 2025)
================================================================================

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS HEADING INTO Q2 2025 EARNINGS:

Wall Street forecasts for Q2 2025 (June Quarter):
- Consensus EPS estimate: $1.90-$2.06
- Consensus revenue estimate: $15.5 billion to $16.16 billion
- UBS analyst Atul Maheswari noted Q2 EPS outlook as "likely to be the key focus"

Market Conditions Pre-Earnings (June 2025):

1. Fuel Cost Headwinds
   - Analysts expected airline to absorb significant fuel cost increases (~$2 billion jump)
   - Q2 represented historically high fuel expense period
   - Pricing power and cost management under scrutiny

2. Premium Demand Expectations
   - Market expected strong premium segment performance
   - Business travel recovery anticipated to support pricing
   - International capacity growth helping offset domestic challenges

3. Full Year 2025 Guidance
   - Prior guidance (January): $6.50-$7.50 EPS for full year 2025 (20% growth over 2024)
   - Q2 expected to set tone for second half of year
   - Free cash flow guidance: $3-$4 billion for full year

4. Industry Dynamics
   - Refinery operations helping support fuel cost management
   - Competition among legacy carriers intensifying
   - Demand bifurcation: premium segments strong, leisure demand moderating

5. Capacity and Revenue Growth
   - International capacity additions expected to support revenue growth
   - Network optimization ongoing
   - Margin expansion potential following Q1 challenges

Key Analyst Questions/Themes:
- Ability to pass through fuel cost increases via pricing
- Sustainability of premium demand
- Second half 2025 demand trajectory
- Operational costs beyond fuel (labor, maintenance)
- Updated guidance for remainder of year
- Free cash flow realization and debt reduction pace
