Delta Air Lines - Pre-Earnings News Digest: Q1 2025 (Report Date: April 9, 2025)
================================================================================

ANALYST EXPECTATIONS HEADING INTO Q1 2025 EARNINGS:

Wall Street forecast for Q1 2025:
- Consensus EPS estimate: $0.38-$0.44 adjusted
- Consensus revenue estimate: $12.98 billion
- Guidance provided in January 2025 for March quarter: Revenue growth 7-9%, EPS $0.70-$1.00

Market Conditions Pre-Earnings (March 2025):

1. Revised Guidance Warning (March 2025)
   - Delta cut first-quarter earnings outlook in early March
   - Cited weaker-than-expected corporate and leisure travel demand
   - This marked a notable shift from earlier optimism about 2025
   - Source: CNBC (https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/09/delta-air-lines-dal-1q-2025-earnings.html)
   - Date: Before April 9, 2025 report

2. Jet Fuel Price Pressures
   - Fuel moved sharply higher in March
   - Q2 fuel expected at approximately $4.30 per gallon (roughly double prior year)
   - Cost management expected to be key focus

3. Capacity and Margin Expectations
   - Market expected capacity reductions in Q2
   - Operating margin guidance: 6-8% expected for Q2
   - Second quarter EPS guidance $1.00-$1.50

4. Operational Performance
   - Delta expected to lead industry in profitability despite macroeconomic headwinds
   - Industry-leading operational reliability anticipated

Key Analyst Questions/Themes:
- Ability to manage through weaker leisure travel demand
- Corporate travel recovery trajectory
- Pricing power amid industry-wide challenges
- Free cash flow generation despite margin pressures
- Balance sheet strength and debt reduction
