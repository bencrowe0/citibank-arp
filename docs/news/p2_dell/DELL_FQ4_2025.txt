DELL TECHNOLOGIES INC - PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST
Q4 FY2026 (Report Date: February 26, 2026)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY
==========================

As Dell Technologies approaches its Q4 FY2026 earnings announcement on February 26, 2026, Wall Street expects a record-breaking finish to a transformational fiscal year. The quarter represents the culmination of Dell's AI infrastructure thesis, with expectations for exceptional profitability, strong guidance for fiscal 2027, and confirmation of AI as a structural growth driver for the company going forward.

ANALYST CONSENSUS & SENTIMENT

General Consensus: Extremely Positive - "Strong Buy" with significant upside potential

Valuation Context:
- Dell expected to finish FY26 as record revenue year and record profitability year
- AI server business scaling to $25B+ annual run rate with multiple expansion potential
- Operating leverage story driving EPS growth significantly above revenue growth

Forward Guidance Expectations:
- FY27 revenue guidance likely $120B+ (20%+ growth) with AI servers as primary driver
- FY27 operating margin guidance expected to show continued improvement
- AI server shipment guidance likely $25B+ for FY27 continuing trajectory

INVESTMENT THESIS FOR Q4
=======================

Bull Case - Primary Market Narrative:
1. AI Infrastructure Supercycle - Multi-year visibility into infrastructure buildout
2. Market Share Gains - Dell gaining share in AI server category vs. competitors
3. Operational Leverage - EPS growth trajectory significantly exceeding revenue growth
4. Margin Expansion - AI server mix improving profitability profile
5. Guidance Momentum - Consistently raising guidance indicating confidence and visibility

Stock Valuation Support:
- Trading at premium multiple to market supported by AI growth narrative
- Analyst price targets suggesting 15-20%+ upside from pre-announcement levels
- Sentiment: Extremely positive heading into announcement

Bear Case - Secondary Considerations:
1. Valuation Risk - Stock priced for perfection on AI momentum continuation
2. Execution Risk - Ability to ramp production and fulfillment to meet demand
3. Competitive Dynamics - Custom chip manufacturers and AMD gaining share
4. Customer Concentration - Hyperscaler revenue concentration reducing diversification
5. Macro Environment - Potential enterprise IT spending slowdown outside AI category

MARKET DYNAMICS HEADING INTO Q4
===============================

1. Technology Sector Momentum
   - Semiconductor and IT infrastructure stocks benefiting from AI buildout
   - Rotation toward infrastructure beneficiaries (Dell, Broadcom, ASML, etc.)
   - Analyst upgrades frequent as AI capex cycle accelerates

2. Enterprise IT Spending
   - CIO budgets increasingly allocated to AI infrastructure vs. traditional IT
   - Hyperscaler capex cycle showing no signs of deceleration
   - Multiple-year visibility on data center AI infrastructure investments

3. Valuation Environment
   - Growth stocks rebounding with Fed rate cut expectations
   - AI narrative providing support for high-growth tech multiples
   - Dell positioned as "pick and shovel" play on AI infrastructure buildout

SEGMENT EXPECTATIONS
====================

Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG) - Primary Growth Driver:
- Expected growth 40%+ YoY driven by AI server surge
- Traditional server business stabilizing
- Networking segment benefiting from data center capex
- Mix shift toward AI servers supporting margin expansion

Client Solutions Group (CSG):
- Expected mid-single-digit growth
- Commercial PC market showing resilience
- Lower growth rate than ISG but stable profitability contribution
- Less economically sensitive than historical PC cycles
