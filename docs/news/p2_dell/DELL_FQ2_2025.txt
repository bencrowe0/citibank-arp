DELL TECHNOLOGIES INC - PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST
Q2 FY2026 (Report Date: August 28, 2025)

MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY
==========================

As Dell Technologies approaches its Q2 FY2026 earnings announcement on August 28, 2025, Wall Street expects substantial acceleration in revenue growth and record profitability, driven primarily by explosive AI server demand. The quarter represents a key inflection point where AI infrastructure investments are translating into massive orders and shipments.

CATALYSTS & DRIVERS
===================

1. AI Infrastructure Buildout
   - Hyperscaler capex cycle for AI training and inference driving server demand
   - Generative AI application adoption accelerating enterprise data center investments
   - Dell positioned as primary beneficiary of infrastructure buildout

2. Record Order Pipeline
   - Extraordinary visibility into demand through record backlog
   - Multiple quarters of strong shipment visibility announced
   - AI server mix expanding rapidly

3. Operational Leverage
   - Volume growth translating to margin expansion
   - Operating expense leverage as company scales
   - EPS growth significantly outpacing revenue growth

4. Market Share Gains
   - Dell gaining share in AI server category vs. traditional competitors
   - Custom ASIC partnerships (NVIDIA, AMD) with Dell positioning

RISK FACTORS TO MONITOR
=======================

1. Valuation Risk: Stock has priced in aggressive growth expectations
2. Execution Risk: Supply chain constraints could limit shipments below guidance
3. Customer Concentration: Dependency on hyperscaler customers for growth
4. Competitive Dynamics: AMD and custom chip manufacturers could disrupt margin structure
5. Macro Environment: Enterprise IT spending cycle turn could impact CSG segment

HISTORICAL CONTEXT & BEAT MAGNITUDE
====================================

- Q2 FY25 (Prior Year): Revenue $25.0B, EPS $1.95
- Expected Growth: +17% revenue, +19% EPS vs. year-ago quarter
- Street Estimate Beat Streak: 4 consecutive quarters of EPS beats
- Guidance Track Record: Dell typically raises guidance in early quarters when visibility improves

SEGMENT ANALYSIS
================

Infrastructure Solutions Group (ISG):
- Expected strong growth driven by AI servers
- Traditional servers stabilizing
- Networking segment benefiting from data center capex

Client Solutions Group (CSG):
- Expected modest growth from commercial PC shipments
- Mixed macro signals on enterprise/commercial PC demand
- Lower mix weight on overall company profitability
