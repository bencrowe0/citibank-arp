RECENT CATALYSTS & MARKET DRIVERS
==================================

1. AI Infrastructure Buildout
   - Continued investment in generative AI applications driving data center capex
   - Demand for AI-optimized servers as foundational infrastructure
   - Dell's positioning as key infrastructure beneficiary

2. Record Order Flow
   - Record $12.1 billion in AI server orders in Q1
   - Strong visibility into demand pipeline for remainder of FY26

3. Profitability Momentum
   - EPS growth (34.7%) significantly outpacing revenue growth (3.9%)
   - Operating leverage improving as company scales at higher volumes

4. Full-Year Confidence
   - Company raising full-year EPS guidance despite macro uncertainty
   - Signals confidence in execution and demand sustainability

RISK FACTORS TO MONITOR
=======================

1. Margin Pressure: Competitive pricing environment could limit gross margin expansion
2. Geographic Mix: Shifting mix toward high-volume lower-margin regions
3. Supply Chain: Continued supply constraints could limit shipment growth
4. Macro Environment: Economic uncertainty and enterprise IT spending slowdown risk
