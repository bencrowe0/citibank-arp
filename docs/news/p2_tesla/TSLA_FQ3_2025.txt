Heading into the October 22, 2025 report, consensus EPS was $0.53-$0.55 (a ~25% YoY decline from $0.72), on revenue of about $26.27-$26.46 billion (+4-6% YoY). Automotive gross margin was expected around 16.5-17.0%. Coverage explicitly framed the setup as a divergence: Tesla was achieving record-ever vehicle deliveries and energy sales for the quarter, yet profitability (EPS, margin) was still expected to decline meaningfully - a "record volume, weaker profit" setup rather than a clean growth story.
