PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST - WORKDAY (WDAY) Q1 FY2026
Report Date: May 22, 2025
Report Period: Q1 FY2026 (ended April 30, 2025)

==============================================================================
ANALYST EXPECTATIONS & MARKET SENTIMENT
==============================================================================

EARNINGS PER SHARE CONSENSUS:
- Expected: $2.52 per share (up from $2.23 prior year)
- Prior Year Q1: $2.23 per share

REVENUE EXPECTATIONS:
- Expected: ~$2.24 billion (based on search results from Benzinga and Wall Street analyst consensus)
- Prior Year Q1: $2.24 billion
- Expected YoY Growth: ~12.3% (in line with 12.6% growth in prior year)

KEY ANALYST COMMENTARY:
- Wall Street consensus reconfirmed estimates over the last 30 days
- Analysts anticipated Workday to "stay the course" heading into earnings
- Mixed sentiment on competitive dynamics and AI market disruption

==============================================================================
KEY BUSINESS DEVELOPMENTS (Pre-Earnings)
==============================================================================

1. AI PRODUCT MOMENTUM
   - AI products contributing approximately 1.5 percentage points to Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) growth
   - AI included in over 75% of net new deals (strong market adoption)
   - Conversations with partners indicated AI was affecting budgets in some cases

2. MARKET SATURATION CONCERNS
   - Slower growth expectations for FY2026 and FY2027 driven by saturation in core Human Capital Management (HCM) market
   - NOT driven by AI disruption concerns
   - Some budget shifts noted as companies prioritize AI and cloud initiatives

3. ANALYST PRICE TARGET MOVEMENTS
   - Cantor Fitzgerald cut price target from $200 to $160 (May 20, 2025)
   - Maintained Overweight rating despite target reduction
   - Stock down approximately 40% YTD as of early May 2025
   - Trading at ~12.3x FY2027 EPS estimate

4. MARKET CONDITIONS
   - Macro environment remained uncertain heading into the quarter
   - Tech sector showing mixed momentum
   - Enterprise software spending cautious but stable for mission-critical systems

==============================================================================
INVESTOR FOCUS AREAS FOR Q1 EARNINGS
==============================================================================

- Subscription revenue trajectory and growth rate sustainability
- AI product contribution breakdown and unit economics
- Forward guidance for FY2026 (full-year subscription revenue guidance)
- Operating margin maintenance amid continued AI investment
- Customer additions and ACV (Annual Contract Value) trends
- Competitive positioning in HCM market
