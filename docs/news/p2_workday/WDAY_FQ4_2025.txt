==============================================================================
KEY BUSINESS DEVELOPMENTS (Pre-Earnings)
==============================================================================

1. LEADERSHIP TRANSITION
   - CEO Carl Eschenbach stepped down (announced February 9, 2026)
   - Aneel Bhusri (co-founder) returning as CEO
   - Introduces near-term uncertainty but also signal of strategic refocus
   - Leadership change creates both risk and potential opportunity for renewed growth initiatives

2. AI PRODUCT MOMENTUM & REVENUE SCALE
   - AI product annualized revenue: >$400 million (exceeded $100M new ACV in Q4 alone)
   - AI ACV growth: 100%+ year-over-year
   - AI included in majority of net new deals (75%+)
   - Emerging AI products contributing 1.5+ points to overall ARR growth

3. GUIDANCE FOR FY2027
   - Company expected to guide FY2027 subscription revenue growth: 12-13% (decelerating from 14% in FY2026)
   - Reflects maturation of core HCM business and market saturation concerns
   - But offset by accelerating AI revenue growth trajectory

4. SUBSCRIPTION REVENUE GROWTH TRAJECTORY
   - Q3 growth: ~14-15% (momentum maintained)
   - Q4 expected growth: ~15-16% (potential acceleration)
   - Full-year FY2026: ~14% growth (stable trajectory)

5. MARGIN OUTLOOK
   - Non-GAAP operating margins expected: ~29-30% range
   - Continued margin expansion expected for FY2027 despite guidance caution
   - Analysts citing AI-driven product advancements and improved operational leverage

6. STRATEGIC ACQUISITIONS & PARTNERSHIPS
   - Paradox, Inc. acquisition (HR service automation/conversational AI) closed in Q3 FY2026
   - Salesforce Financial Management adoption (closed in Q2) validating multi-suite strategy
   - Major customer wins in healthcare, financial services, and public sector
