PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST - WORKDAY (WDAY) Q3 FY2026
Report Date: November 25, 2025
Report Period: Q3 FY2026 (ended October 31, 2025)

==============================================================================
ANALYST EXPECTATIONS & MARKET SENTIMENT
==============================================================================

EARNINGS PER SHARE CONSENSUS:
- Expected: ~$2.17-$2.18 per share (adjusted/non-GAAP)
- Prior Year Q3: ~$1.76 per share (implied ~23-25% YoY growth expected)

REVENUE EXPECTATIONS:
- Expected: ~$2.42 billion
- Prior Year Q3: ~$2.15 billion
- Expected Growth: ~11.8% YoY

GUIDANCE & FORWARD EXPECTATIONS:
- Full-year FY2026 subscription revenue guidance maintained at ~$8.828 billion
- FY2027 growth expected: ~13% (below FY2026's ~14.4%)
- Operating margin guidance for Q4: estimated 28.7% (StreetAccount consensus)

KEY ANALYST VIEWS (Pre-Earnings):
- Analysts generally reconfirmed estimates over the last 30 days
- Mixed sentiment on future growth trajectory
- Macro headwinds from potential trade policy changes noted
- Concerns about corporate tax policy impact on business confidence

==============================================================================
KEY BUSINESS DEVELOPMENTS (Pre-Earnings)
==============================================================================

1. AI PRODUCT MOMENTUM CONTINUES
   - AI-driven solutions showing accelerating adoption
   - AI products contributing ~1.5 points to ARR growth
   - Integrated AI features across HCM and Financial Management platforms
   - Over 75% of net new deals including AI components

2. SUBSCRIPTION REVENUE GROWTH TRAJECTORY
   - Q2 subscription revenue growth: 14% (accelerating from Q1)
   - Market expectations for Q3: 13-14% range
   - Full-year FY2026 guidance: 14% growth (implies consistent mid-teens growth)

3. MARGIN ENVIRONMENT & INVESTMENT
   - Non-GAAP operating margins stable at ~28-29% range
   - Continued investments in AI product development
   - Professional services margins under pressure (expected for software companies)

4. MACRO & COMPETITIVE CONTEXT
   - Macro uncertainty from potential trade policy changes
   - Corporate tax environment discussions affecting business confidence
   - Tech sector showing mixed momentum entering holiday season
   - Competitive pressures from legacy ERP vendors adding AI capabilities

5. CUSTOMER EXPANSION INITIATIVES
   - Financial Management product gaining traction (Salesforce went live in Q2)
   - Success in SLED (State, Local, Education) segment expanding
   - Healthcare vertical momentum continuing
   - International expansion through cloud infrastructure (e.g., UK AWS launch in Q1)

==============================================================================
INVESTOR FOCUS AREAS FOR Q3 EARNINGS
==============================================================================

- Q3 subscription revenue growth vs. Q2's 14% (confirmation of momentum)
- Detailed AI revenue contribution and unit economics
- Full-year FY2026 guidance reaffirmation or revision
- FY2027 guidance initialization (critical for valuation)
- Operating margin performance vs. guidance
- Enterprise customer wins and average deal size trends
- Professional services revenue and profitability
- Free cash flow generation and capital allocation

==============================================================================
MARKET CONDITIONS HEADING INTO EARNINGS
==============================================================================

- U.S. macro environment uncertain due to potential policy changes
- Technology sector valuations recovering from earlier 2025 weakness
- Enterprise software sector showing resilience in mission-critical segments
- Increased focus on AI implementation ROI by enterprise customers
- Competitive intensity in HCM and Finance automation markets intensifying
