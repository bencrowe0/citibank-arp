PRE-EARNINGS NEWS DIGEST - WORKDAY (WDAY) Q2 FY2026
Report Date: August 21, 2025
Report Period: Q2 FY2026 (ended July 31, 2025)

==============================================================================
ANALYST EXPECTATIONS & MARKET SENTIMENT
==============================================================================

EARNINGS PER SHARE CONSENSUS:
- Expected: ~$2.11-$2.21 per share
- Prior Year Q2: $1.95 per share (implied ~8-13% YoY growth)

REVENUE EXPECTATIONS:
- Expected: ~$2.35 billion
- Prior Year Q2: ~$2.08 billion
- Expected Growth: ~12.6-13% YoY

KEY ANALYST VIEWS (Pre-Earnings):
- Street research showed cautious sentiment after Q4/Q1 results
- Broad reset of expectations across analyst community
- Price targets saw multiple cuts ranging from $160-$184+
- Concerns about decelerating sales growth and softer long-term guidance

==============================================================================
KEY BUSINESS DEVELOPMENTS (Pre-Earnings)
==============================================================================

1. AI ACCELERATION
   - AI product ACV (Annual Contract Value) surging over 200% year-over-year
   - AI generating approximately 1.5 points of ARR growth
   - Over 75% of net new deals including AI components
   - Market seeing strong adoption of Workday's AI-powered tools

2. ANALYST SENTIMENT & PRICE TARGETS
   - Updated price target moves lower to $184.73 from $231.17 (prior consensus)
   - Multiple analyst cuts citing:
     * Decelerating sales expectations
     * Softer long-term guidance
     * Higher investments in AI development
     * Leadership changes (CEO transition context mentioned)
   - Street research turn: more cautious positioning

3. COMPETITIVE DYNAMICS
   - Continued pressure from broader enterprise software market
   - AI disruption impacting customer budget allocation in some segments
   - Saturation in core HCM market remains concern
   - Success in expanding beyond traditional HCM into financial management seen as positive

4. STRATEGIC DEVELOPMENTS
   - Acquisition announcement: Paradox, Inc. majority stake (definitive agreement signed August 21, 2025)
   - Expected to close Q3 FY2026 (by October 31, 2025)
   - Move signals expansion into HR service automation/conversational AI

==============================================================================
INVESTOR FOCUS AREAS FOR Q2 EARNINGS
==============================================================================

- Q2 subscription revenue growth rate vs. Q1 performance
- Confirmation of AI product momentum and contribution to revenue
- Paradox acquisition rationale and expected contribution
- Operating margin guidance (potential for margin compression from AI investments)
- Full-year FY2026 guidance updates or reaffirmation
- Professional services revenue and mix shifts
- Customer wins in key verticals (Financial Services, Healthcare, Public Sector)

==============================================================================
MARKET CONDITIONS HEADING INTO EARNINGS
==============================================================================

- U.S. macro environment showing mixed signals
- Technology sector valuations under pressure
- Enterprise software spending moderating but stable for essential systems
- AI investment cycle creating both opportunities and budget pressures
