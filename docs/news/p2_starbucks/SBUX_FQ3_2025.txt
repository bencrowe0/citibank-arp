STARBUCKS Q3 FISCAL 2025 (FQ3 2025) PRE-EARNINGS DIGEST
Report Date: July 29, 2025
Compiled: July 28, 2025

ANALYST CONSENSUS EXPECTATIONS
==============================
Earnings Per Share (EPS): $0.64-$0.66 (adjusted)
Revenue: $9.1-$9.3 billion
Year-over-Year Growth: 2% revenue growth expected
Comparable Store Sales: Estimated -1.3% (modest decline expected after prior quarters' softness)

KEY PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT
==================================

TURNAROUND MOMENTUM BUILDING
https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/07/28/is-starbuckss-stock-a-buy-ahead-of-its-q3-earnings-report-tomorrow/
Motley Fool analysts note that Starbucks' "Back to Starbucks" strategy, including simplified menus and faster wait times, showed momentum in Q2 with global comparable sales rising 6.2% and U.S. comparable sales increasing 7.1%. Market is watching whether Q3 builds on this momentum.

VALUATION AND MOMENTUM CONCERNS
https://finance.yahoo.com/news/starbuckss-stock-buy-ahead-q3-180746002.html
Yahoo Finance pre-earnings analysis highlights that while the stock has rallied nearly 26% year-to-date on turnaround optimism, investors are cautious. Historically, SBUX stock has underperformed after earnings releases 55% of the time, with median one-day declines of 4.4%.

STOCK REACTION PATTERNS SUGGEST CAUTION
https://www.forbes.com/sites/greatspeculations/2025/07/28/how-will-starbucks-stock-react-to-its-upcoming-earnings/
Forbes Great Speculations examines how SBUX stock typically reacts to earnings announcements, noting that strong guidance is needed to sustain recent gains despite the prior quarter's positive momentum.

CHINA MARKET STRUCTURAL WEAKNESS
https://www.cnbc.com/2025/02/26/starbucks-is-struggling-to-grow-sales-in-china-heres-why.html
CNBC reports on Starbucks' ongoing China challenges. The company faces a structural price-war problem as Luckin Coffee undercuts on price by 30-40%, and Chinese consumers face economic pressure amid broader economic slowdown concerns. This headwind expected to persist into Q3 results.

LABOR AND COST PRESSURES
Starbucks' July 2025 announcement of a $500 million labor investment initiative to raise store partner wages exceeded market expectations, creating margin pressure heading into Q3. With coffee commodity costs and new operating model expenses, operating margins expected to contract despite revenue growth.

MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY
============================
- Q3 expected to show modest revenue growth (2% YoY) but modest profitability pressure
- Comparable store sales likely to remain challenged (-1%-2% range) despite Q2 momentum
- Investors watching closely for management commentary on China weakness and margin trajectory
- Guidance on FY2025 profitability and the sustainability of the turnaround strategy critical
