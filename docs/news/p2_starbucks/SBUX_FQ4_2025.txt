STARBUCKS Q4 FISCAL 2025 (FQ4 2025) PRE-EARNINGS DIGEST
Report Date: October 29, 2025
Compiled: October 28, 2025

ANALYST CONSENSUS EXPECTATIONS
==============================
Earnings Per Share (EPS): $0.55-$0.57 (adjusted)
Revenue: $9.35-$9.40 billion
Year-over-Year Growth: 2.6% revenue growth expected
U.S. Comparable Store Sales: Flat to slightly negative
China Comparable Store Sales: +2.2% expected (modest improvement)

KEY PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT
==================================

TURNAROUND CREDIBILITY TEST: SAME-STORE SALES RECOVERY
https://www.indexbox.io/blog/starbucks-fiscal-q4-2025-earnings-report/
IndexBox analysis titled "Starbucks Q4 2025 Earnings: Turnaround Under Scrutiny" examines whether the Back to Starbucks turnaround strategy can deliver same-store sales stabilization. Q3 saw a -2% comp sales decline, making Q4 a critical test of whether the strategy is resonating with customers across U.S. and international markets.

VISITATION TRENDS REMAIN UNDER PRESSURE
https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/earnings-preview-expect-starbucks-report-181527744.html
Yahoo Finance earnings preview reports that analyst concerns center on whether customer visitation trends have improved. Stifel analyst Chris O'Cull noted that "visitation trends do not appear to have improved" based on early-quarter data, making same-store sales recovery during the holiday season "crucial for near-term share price performance."

MARGIN COMPRESSION AND COST HEADWINDS
https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/29/starbucks-sbux-earnings-q4-2025.html
CNBC Q4 earnings analysis highlights significant margin pressure from:
- Labor investments ($500M+ in wage increases)
- Elevated coffee commodity costs
- Store renovation and Siren System equipment costs
- Competitive promotional activity required to maintain traffic
Operating margins expected to remain compressed versus historical levels.

CHINA COMPETITIVE INTENSITY REMAINS STRUCTURAL
https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ueberblick/starbucks-stock-trades-steadily-as-investors-weigh-slower-china-trends-and/69784372
Ad Hoc News reports on China market dynamics as investors assess Starbucks' ability to stabilize market share against aggressive local competitors. Luckin Coffee's pricing advantage and expanding footprint continue to pressure Starbucks' average ticket in China, though transaction volume has shown modest stabilization.

HOLIDAY SEASON PERFORMANCE CRITICAL
Holiday quarter (Q4 includes September through November) typically represents one of Starbucks' strongest periods seasonally. Market expectations hinge on whether festive menu offerings and the loyalty program can drive traffic growth, particularly given labor availability and wage cost headwinds.

MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY
============================
- Q4 will likely miss on earnings per share (-3% vs. estimates) due to margin compression
- Revenue expected to beat slightly, driven by pricing discipline and international strength
- Critical question: Do same-store sales turn positive for first time in multiple quarters?
- Guidance for FY2026 profitability recovery and operating margin trajectory will drive stock reaction
- Investors waiting to hear specific plans to expand margins while maintaining customer experience investments
