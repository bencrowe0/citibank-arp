STARBUCKS Q1 FISCAL 2026 (FQ1 2026) PRE-EARNINGS DIGEST
Report Date: January 27, 2026
Compiled: January 26, 2026

ANALYST CONSENSUS EXPECTATIONS
==============================
Earnings Per Share (EPS): $0.59 (expected to decline 14.5% YoY from $0.69 in Q1 FY2025)
Revenue: ~$9.4-9.5 billion (modest growth expected)
Comparable Store Sales: Modest positive growth or flat expected
Fiscal 2026 Full-Year EPS Guidance: $2.15-$2.35 consensus (slower growth relative to pre-turnaround)

KEY PRE-EARNINGS MARKET SENTIMENT
==================================

TURNAROUND PROVING ITS MERIT, BUT AT PROFIT COST
https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36793116/starbucks-q1-2026-earnings-what-to-expect
Barchart analysis heading into Q1 2026 indicates that Starbucks' Back to Starbucks strategy continues to stabilize traffic after multiple quarters of decline. However, the strategy's success in driving transactions comes at significant cost, as heavy labor investments and equipment spending in stores compress near-term profitability despite revenue growth.

TRAFFIC IMPROVEMENT EXPECTED BUT MARGINS UNDER PRESSURE
https://www.cnbc.com/2026/01/28/starbucks-is-about-to-report-earnings-heres-what-to-expect.html
CNBC reports that Q1 FY2026 comparable store sales likely show positive growth (estimated +2%-3%), marking the first quarter of meaningful traffic recovery in two years. However, this growth masks significant operating margin compression to approximately 11%-12% (down from 16%+ in prior-year Q1), driven by elevated labor costs (now averaging $30/hour for partners with benefits), new Siren System equipment capital, and coffee commodity inflation.

PROFITABILITY RECOVERY DELAYED, INVESTORS DEMAND TIMELINE
https://markets.financialcontent.com/wral/article/predictstreet-2025-12-8-starbucks-sbux-navigating-a-complex-brew-of-turnaround-and-global-challenges
PredictStreet/FinancialContent analysis notes that while CEO Brian Niccol's "Back to Starbucks" strategy is winning back customer preference, Wall Street remains skeptical about the timeline and magnitude of profitability recovery. The company projects operating margins to expand from ~9% currently to 13.5%-15% by late 2026, but investors are demanding proof that scale and operational efficiency can bridge this gap without sacrificing the experience improvements.

CEO'S FIRST FULL QUARTER STRATEGIC FOCUS
CEO Brian Niccol (joined late in FY2025) has emphasized repositioning Starbucks around the in-store customer experience rather than mobile-order optimization. This involves new beverage platforms, equipment investments, and staffing to improve drink quality and wait times. Q1 will be market's first chance to assess the full impact of these strategic shifts on both traffic and profitability.

INTERNATIONAL PERFORMANCE CRITICAL TO OFFSET U.S. MARGIN PRESSURE
Q1 FY2026 will reveal whether international markets (particularly Japan, UK, Mexico) can deliver higher-margin growth to offset U.S. margin compression. This geographic mix is critical to justify the heavy U.S. investments to investors.

DIVIDEND AND CAPITAL ALLOCATION SCRUTINY
Starbucks announced a quarterly dividend of $0.62/share in December 2025, but with lower earnings and free cash flow pressures, analysts will scrutinize whether the company can sustain dividend growth while funding store renovations and labor investments.

MARKET EXPECTATIONS SUMMARY
============================
- Q1 FY2026 expected to show traffic stabilization (comps +2%-3%) but EPS down ~14% YoY
- Operating margins compressed significantly (~11%-12%) due to labor and equipment investments
- Investors will focus heavily on FY2026 guidance and management's profitability recovery timeline
- Critical question: Can Starbucks expand margins to 13-15% by end of 2026 while maintaining experience investments?
- Near-term profitability headwinds expected to persist; long-term strategy credibility on trial
- Margin expansion, China stabilization, and international performance will determine stock trajectory
