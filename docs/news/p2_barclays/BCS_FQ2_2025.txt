Barclays (BCS) - News digest, FQ2 2025 / H1 2025 interim results (report date 2025-07-29). Pre-earnings estimate context only.

Ahead of Barclays' H1 2025 interim results (announced 29 July 2025), analyst consensus called for first-half pre-tax profit of approximately £5.94 billion, with second-quarter equities/investment-banking trading revenue in focus after a strong Q1. Coverage in the run-up to the print flagged Barclays' Investment Bank division as the key swing factor, following peer Wall Street banks' own strong trading results earlier in the reporting season, and noted the market would be watching for an update to the bank's FY2025/2026 income guidance (previously ~£31bn for 2026) and progress on its cost-efficiency and capital-return (buyback) targets.
